Summary

Developer framework and managed platform for ingestion, parsing, indexing, retrieval and knowledge agents, including LlamaParse for documents and a property-graph index for graph-structured retrieval.

Our take

The default toolkit for building the layer this catalog describes, which makes it both the enabler of the whole category and the reason none of the middle of it is defensible. Anything expressible as a retrieval pattern ends up in here free within a year.
