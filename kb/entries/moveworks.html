Summary

Conversational AI assistant, enterprise search and reasoning engine for employee service and IT helpdesk. ServiceNow announced its acquisition in March 2025 at a reported $2.85B and completed it on 15 December 2025 after a US Justice Department antitrust review.

Our take

ServiceNow's largest acquisition to date, and the clearest instance of the pattern: the standalone knowledge assistant was absorbed by the system of record that already held the workflow, the tickets and the entitlements.

Key numbers

  • $2.85B reported deal value; ServiceNow did not confirm a figure at closing (press)
  • Combined deployment cited at 5.5M employees across 250 shared customers (vendor-reported)
  • Nearly 90% of Moveworks customers had deployed to their entire workforce (vendor-reported)
  • HQ: Mountain View, California
