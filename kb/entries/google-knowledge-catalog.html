Summary

Dataplex renamed Knowledge Catalog in April 2026 and marketed as "the universal context engine for your enterprise". Rather than asking customers to adopt a new definition language, since 30 October 2026 it ingests other tools' definitions and combines them into one context graph: Looker LookML, BigQuery measures, dbt MetricFlow, Snowflake Semantic Views, Databricks Metric Views and SAP's data-model notation. Knowledge also enters through Gemini extraction from documents, generated table and column descriptions, joins mined from query history, and a preview "self-learning enrichment" that finds missing or outdated context.

Our take

The most distinctive conflict policy in the whole tier, and the right one: when two tools define the same metric differently, both definitions appear with their provenance for a steward to reconcile, and nothing is silently overwritten. Every other vendor resolves conflicts automatically with a precedence rule or a score; Google shows the conflict to a person. Positioning itself as the place where everyone else's semantic layers meet is also the only strategy here that does not require winning the definition war.

Key numbers

  • "Nearly 4X more accurate, up to 33% lower latency, up to 75% fewer tokens" (vendor-reported, no published method)
  • Bloomberg Media: 102 auto-generated usage notes and a 63% improvement in SQL accuracy (vendor-reported, via search results only)
