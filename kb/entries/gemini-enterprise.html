Summary

Google's enterprise AI workplace platform, announced in October 2025 as the rename of Agentspace, its enterprise search and agent product. New purchases of standalone Agentspace licences ended after 31 December 2025, and at Cloud Next in April 2026 Vertex AI was renamed the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform with Agentspace folded in as a feature.

Our take

Agentspace existed as a named enterprise search product for roughly a year before becoming a feature of a model subscription. That is the clearest hyperscaler version of the absorption pattern, and it happened faster than any of the acquisitions.
