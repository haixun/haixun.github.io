Microsoft Fabric IQ

Summary

Business-context layer over Fabric and OneLake comprising an Ontology item, Power BI semantic models, a Graph, and data and operations agents. The Ontology defines entity types, properties, relationships, rules and policies and binds them to data; Fabric then derives a graph child item from those bindings. Introduced at Ignite 2025, where Ontology was the genuinely new component.

Our take

The entry that invalidates the comfortable reading of Microsoft as a company with the corpus and no ontology. It has an explicit ontology product, it shares RDF ontologies, and at Ignite 2026 it shipped Fabric IQ connectors for ChatGPT and Claude — exposing its own semantic layer to rival models rather than fencing it in. The Ontology item has nonetheless been in preview for a year, which is the honest measure of how hard this is even with Microsoft's resources.

Key numbers

  • Ontology in preview since November 2025, with general availability promised in the coming months as of Ignite 2026 (vendor-reported)
  • Sources disagree on whether the wider Fabric IQ workload is generally available or still preview (press, inconsistent)
