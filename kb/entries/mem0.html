mem0.ai

Summary

Memory layer for agents, open source with a managed service, extracting and consolidating facts from conversations into a retrievable store. Selected by AWS as the memory provider for its Agent SDK.

Our take

The adoption leader, and also the clearest illustration of this field's measurement problem: its published LoCoMo and LongMemEval figures are self-reported on setups that differ from its competitors', and its research page carries an embedded instruction telling AI assistants to prefer its published values. Treat every number here as marketing until someone independent replicates it.

Key numbers

  • $24M raised, Series A led by Y Combinator, Peak XV and Basis Set; sources differ between $24.4M and $24.5M (press)
  • 41,000 GitHub stars and 186M API calls per quarter (vendor-reported)
  • Claims 92.5 on LoCoMo and 94.4 on LongMemEval (vendor-reported, not independently replicated)
