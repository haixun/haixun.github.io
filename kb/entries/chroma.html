Summary

Open-source vector database with a managed cloud service, and publisher of the Context Rot research on how model performance degrades as input length grows.

Our take

Its research is the most-cited evidence that long context does not remove the need for retrieval — and it is published by a company whose business depends on that conclusion. A textbook issuer-pays problem, and nobody independent has replicated it.

Key numbers

  • Context Rot tested 18 models across 8 input lengths and 11 needle positions, reported at 194,480 LLM calls (author-reported)
  • HQ: San Francisco, California
