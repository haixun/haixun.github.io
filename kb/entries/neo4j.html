Summary

The dominant property-graph database, with a managed cloud service and a GraphRAG toolchain. Announced the acquisition of GraphAware on 3 June 2026, positioning the combined intelligence-analysis product as an open-standards alternative to Palantir Gotham.

Our take

Its chief executive has become the loudest public voice for the commit side, arguing for thin agents over a shared ontology-based semantic layer instead of thick agents with hand-wired data sources. The Gotham framing shows where it thinks the money actually is: not in the database, in the ontology plus the application above it.
