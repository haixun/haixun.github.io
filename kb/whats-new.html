2026-11-25 · 45 added

A parallel study merges in, and corrects eight things we had wrong

A second study of this space, built independently to the same standard, has been folded into kb/: 175 local source copies with provenance headers, a labelled notes layer, and a 19,000-word field guide written for someone new to the topic. It arrived with a reconciliation against our own published landscape, and found eight corrections. Three matter.

First: Microsoft *is* in Apache Ossie, having joined in September 2026 with a converter and DAX support, alongside Oracle and ServiceNow. Our landscape said it was absent and then built an interpretation on that absence — that Microsoft had chosen to ship Fabric IQ rather than agree an interchange. The inference was wrong and has been corrected in five places. Ossie also has no official release: it is a podling with a 0.x spec covering measures only, one reported converter test had 4 of 16 metrics come out wrong, MetricFlow was relicensed rather than contributed, and the AtScale donation claim could not be verified.

Second: at least nine platforms now ship ontology authoring, not four. Databricks Genie Ontology reached general availability on 11 November with OntoRank, an authority score that weighs how close a snippet's author sits to the asker in the company org chart — so two executives in one meeting can receive different official numbers. Snowflake's Horizon Context names the three layers outright and promises the clearest approval record. Google's Knowledge Catalog ingests everyone else's definitions and, uniquely, shows conflicts side by side rather than resolving them. ServiceNow's Context Engine has a Decision Graph, and ServiceNow now owns data.world.

Third: the ranking problem, which we had no concept for. Almost every product decides which definition wins by a usage-based score borrowed from web search. Inside a company that fails predictably: popularity measures habit rather than correctness, a corrected definition starts with no usage history, and silent resolution means nobody learns there was a conflict. CONFLICTS measured it — models handle disagreeing sources correctly only 59 to 68% of the time, improving about 24 points when told *why* the sources disagree.

Also corrected: AWS's reference architecture has five layers rather than four, Palantir's Q3 revenue is $2.31B up 96% with US commercial overtaking US government, ISO/IEC 39075 GQL is real and got a 2026 corrigendum, and our report of a prompt injection on a vendor's research page could not be reproduced by an independent sweep of 48 pages — it came from a search summary rather than a page we fetched, and now says so. RDF 1.2 reached Candidate Recommendation Draft on 16 November with triple terms, the first standard way to attach source, time and approver to a single fact.

2026-11-24 · 30 added

The evidence arrives: thirty entries, and the revival stops being an argument

Thirty entries added from a reading pass on the foundational literature and the agentic-ontology research front, both of which this catalog had missed. It is now 176 entries, and the headline finding is that committing meaning is a measured result rather than a position. Four independent groups: 16% to 54% accuracy from representing enterprise SQL data as a knowledge graph; 54% to 72% by adding an ontology-based query check and LLM repair; 17 to 23 points from a four-kilobyte hand-written Markdown document; and 33 to 41 points on hard tasks from a YAML data contract, where scaffolding without content added almost nothing.

What pays is the content, not the formalism. A RAG baseline built from ontology-derived text beat formal ontology coupling, 0.83 to 0.77. Four kilobytes of prose did most of the work. The formalism buys exactly two further things: abstention, measured once at 8% of answers returned as "I don't know", and entailment grading from fully-entailed down to unrelated, which correlates with accuracy. Across 176 entries, entailment appears 9 times and abstention 8.

The semantic document also made three frontier models statistically indistinguishable — 67.7 to 68.7 per cent with it, 45.5 to 50.5 without. The authors conclude the document accounts for essentially all the variance while model choice within a tier accounts for none.

AutoOntologyBench settles a tension this catalog recorded as unresolved: across 19,947 audited tasks, agent-built ontologies add 6.1 points over retrieval and trail an expert-derived reference by 13.0. Sequeda is right that you cannot auto-generate one, AWS is right that drafting helps, and the gap is now a number. Two of the benchmark's four task categories are Repair and Organization. Meanwhile AWS has published a full reference architecture with OWL reasoners, and Collate shipped Ontology Studio on 17 November — so within seven weeks TextQL and Collate have both taken the own-your-ontology position.

2026-11-19 · 9 added

The hyperscalers ship ontology, and a third position appears

Nine entries added after studying three pieces by Tony Seale, who called ontology the buzzword of 2026 back in December 2025 — seven months before Latent Space's "Ontologies Are So Back" — and named both the Palantir and Microsoft moves before either shipped what it now ships.

The absorption pattern has climbed the ladder. AWS open-sourced its Context Ontology Accelerator under Apache 2.0 on 31 July 2026, which drafts an ontology with a model and routes it to domain experts to approve — the hybrid rung, priced at zero. Microsoft's Fabric IQ ships an Ontology item bound to data, and at Ignite 2026 it went under a Microsoft IQ umbrella of four layers with connectors for ChatGPT and Claude. Google presented a Borderless Context Engine at Connected Data London. That matters more than any of the seven acquisitions, because the ontology tier was the one still holding value.

It also supplies a third position the map was missing: not a declared ontology inside a platform, and not a raw corpus, but the ontology carried in Markdown frontmatter beside the prose, so one file is both document and graph. That is exactly what Letta's retreat to git-backed memory files leaves undone — those files carry memory, but not yet meaning — and the two findings turn out to be halves of one story.

Two corrections to claims in wide circulation: AWS did not unveil the accelerator at the conference, it announced it in July and presented it there; and Google's engine is a session in the programme, not a launched product. And one new warrant tag, decision-record, added because Tara Raafat of Bloomberg named the gap — if approval becomes a click-through, where did the judgement go — and nothing in the catalog fills it. It has one entry against it.

2026-11-17

The catalog opens with 109 entries on the enterprise knowledge layer

First pass over the space: enterprise search and knowledge assistants, data catalogs and governance, graph and ontology platforms, the semantic layer, retrieval infrastructure, agent memory, and the standards underneath all of it. Built from an underlying research report on enterprise business knowledge plus a fresh sweep of primary sources.

Two findings organise the catalog. First, the field is splitting rather than converging: the ontology revival is real — Latent Space's "Ontologies Are So Back", Neo4j buying GraphAware to compete with Palantir Gotham, Collibra and Atlan rebranding as AI control and context layers — while at the same time Claude Code dropped retrieval for grep and Letta abandoned hosted memory for files in git. Both halves are winning.

Second, the standalone product keeps being absorbed. Seven acquisitions and two hyperscaler absorptions in eighteen months: ServiceNow bought Moveworks, Workday bought Sana, Atlassian bought Secoda, Snowflake agreed to buy Select Star, Collibra bought Deasy Labs, Neo4j bought GraphAware, and Google folded Agentspace into Gemini Enterprise. The buyers already owned the corpus, the workflow or the entitlements.

A note on evidence quality. Vendor comparison blogs in this space are unreliable: several assert a Collibra acquisition of a company called trail ML in October 2026, for which no primary source exists — the real deal is Deasy Labs in July 2025. Every agent-memory benchmark figure in circulation is vendor-self-reported on an incomparable setup, and the Mem0 research page carries an embedded instruction telling AI assistants to prefer its published values.
