Summary

A 'harness foundry' that assembles typed harness primitives through a substitution algebra over nine dimensions, adapts them with AEGIS, a trace-driven multi-agent evolution engine, and turns trajectories into both harness updates and model training signal. Evaluated on ALFWorld, GAIA, WebShop, τ³-Bench and SWE-bench Verified.

Our take

Infrastructure framing of harness search with typed, swappable components, which is closer to a physical-plan space than most. Gains are largest where baselines are weakest, so read them as headroom, not ceiling.

Key numbers

  • Average gain +14.5% (up to +44.0%) across five benchmarks (author-reported)
