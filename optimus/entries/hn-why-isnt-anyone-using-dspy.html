Summary

Discussion (227 points, 120 comments) of Skylar Payne's essay on DSPy engineering patterns. Practitioners say optimisation pays off only with a good automated metric and labelled data that take much upfront work, and worry about framework lock-in and optimised prompts that cannot easily be taken out.

Our take

The user-side explanation for why per-call optimisers stay niche: nobody has written the metric, and the output is not portable. Those are the two gaps we sell into.
