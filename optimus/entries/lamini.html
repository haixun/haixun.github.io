Summary

Enterprise platform for fine-tuning and running open LLMs in-house, known for 'Memory Tuning' aimed at factual recall and for running on AMD GPUs. Raised $25M (seed plus Series A, led by First Round and Amplify) in 2024. On 17 November 2026 the lamini.ai site fails over HTTPS while the docs site still loads, and one blog reports the CEO now lists herself at AMD.

Our take

Looks like a fine-tuning specialist fading out without an announcement, the same quiet pattern as TensorZero; treat as probable graveyard until confirmed.
