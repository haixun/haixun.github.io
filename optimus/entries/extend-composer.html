Summary

Agent that optimises document-processing schemas (field descriptions, extraction rules) for extraction, classification and splitting. It tests candidate schemas against the user's eval set, records field-level gains and regressions, runs experiments in parallel and returns diffs and reasoning for one-click acceptance. Live for all Extend customers from 30 September 2025.

Our take

Declare-and-optimise that works because the metric is field accuracy against ground truth and the scope is one step. It is the per-step optimiser we would call, not the program-level one we would build.

Key numbers

  • Some early-beta teams reached '99% accuracy on challenging document tasks in under 10 minutes' (vendor-reported)
