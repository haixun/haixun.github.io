Summary

Searches over combinations of inference-time techniques (repeated sampling, ranking, fusion, critique, verification) and LLMs under a compute budget, finding architectures on the accuracy-versus-token-budget frontier for target benchmarks.

Our take

Shows physical plans built from inference-time operators can beat single frontier models, so the operator library matters; benchmark-tuned, with no notion of a customer program.

Key numbers

  • Outperforms o1, GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet by 15.1% on average across instruction-following, reasoning and coding (author-reported)
