Summary

Post-training and continual-learning platform: the customer brings tasks, a harness and a grader; Trajectory runs RL (GSPO or GRPO, LoRA on Thinking Machines' Tinker), compares checkpoints, serves the winner on an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and retrains on production feedback. Self-serve platform launched 2 November 2026; production learning and weight export are private beta.

Our take

The nearest funded thing to 'domain experts define good, the machine improves the system', but it only ever answers with RL on weights and keeps the trace loop on its own endpoint. Its own finding that a one-line judge gave no improvement supports the thesis that the grader, not the optimiser, is the hard part.
