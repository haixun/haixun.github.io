Summary

Comment by TensorZero's co-founder in the thread 'AI OSS tool repo goes archived over night after raising $7.3M Seed', explaining that the team decided to wind down, had spent less than half its seed, and would return the rest to investors; the Apache 2.0 repository stays on GitHub unmaintained. Later replies cite usage and the difficulty of finding product-market fit twice as an open-source company.

Our take

Primary evidence that TensorZero's end was a company decision about open-source economics and pace, not a verdict on automated optimisation.
