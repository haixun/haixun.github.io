Meta Context Engineering via Agentic Skill Evolution

author-reported

arxiv.org/abs/2601.21557
code: github.com/metaevo-ai/meta-context-engineering

People Haoran Ye, Xuning He, Vincent Arak, Haonan Dong, Guojie Song

Summary

A bi-level framework: a meta-level agent evolves context-engineering skills by agentic crossover over the history of skills, executions and evaluations, while a base-level agent executes them and optimises context as files and code. Evaluated across five domains, offline and online.

Our take

The clearest instance of optimising two rungs of the ladder at once (the optimiser and the context). It replaces hand-built context-engineering harnesses such as ACE rather than questioning how gains are measured.

Key numbers

  • 5.6–53.8% relative improvement over state-of-the-art agentic context-engineering methods, mean 16.9% (author-reported)
