People Jiale Lao, Andreas Zimmerer, Olga Ovcharenko, Tianji Cong, Matthew Russo, Gerardo Vitagliano, Immanuel Trummer, et al.

Summary

A benchmark for SQL engines extended with LLM-evaluated semantic operators, varying scenario, modality (text, images, audio) and operator (filter, join, map, rank, classify). It evaluates LOTUS, Palimpzest, ThalamusDB and Google BigQuery.

Our take

The first shared yardstick for the semantic-operator line, so claims such as Abacus's can be compared; it scores systems, not certificates.
