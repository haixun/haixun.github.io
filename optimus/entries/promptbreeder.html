Promptbreeder: Self-Referential Self-Improvement Via Prompt Evolution

author-reported

promptsearchmetricgeneralprompt-optimisationself-improvement

arxiv.org/abs/2309.16797

People Chrisantha Fernando, Dylan Banarse, Henryk Michalewski, Simon Osindero, Tim Rocktäschel

Summary

An LLM evolves a population of task prompts, evaluated for fitness on a training set, where the mutation prompts that drive evolution are themselves evolved. Outperforms Chain-of-Thought and Plan-and-Solve prompting on arithmetic and commonsense benchmarks.

Our take

The prompt-level ancestor of self-referential optimisation; a reference for the bottom rung of the ladder rather than for workflow optimisation.
