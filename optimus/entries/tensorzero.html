Summary

Open-source (Apache 2.0) LLMOps stack: gateway, observability, evaluation, optimisation and A/B testing. In March 2026 it announced Autopilot, 'an automated AI engineer' that set up evals and judges from production data, optimised prompts and models and ran adaptive A/B tests. The company wound down in June 2026 and returned remaining capital to investors; the repository was archived.

Our take

Often cited as proof that automatic optimisation fails, but it was an LLMOps company whose Autopilot bet was eleven weeks old when it closed; the founder blamed open-source economics and pace. The real lesson is that a standalone optimiser without the customer's data or runtime has no anchor.
