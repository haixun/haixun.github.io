Summary

Production monitoring for AI agents ('Sentry for AI agents'): traces runs, flags silent failures such as wrong answers, tool loops and frustrated users, and tracks custom behaviours as Signals. Its Simulations product, announced with a CRV-led Series A in September 2026, runs on every pull request and replays production traffic and test cases against a harness change with anomaly detection before it ships.

Our take

An observability vendor moving toward the acceptance gate: replaying production traffic against a change is the right test, but anomaly detection is not a certificate and Raindrop proposes no changes itself.
