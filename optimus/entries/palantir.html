Summary

Public (NYSE: PLTR) data and AI platform company whose Foundry Ontology models an enterprise's objects, actions and logic, and whose AIP layer runs LLM workflows on top of it. AIP Evolve coordinates 'fleets of AI FDE agents' that propose and validate changes (model migration, cost, latency, eval score) to AI systems built in Foundry.

Our take

The closest commercial match to optimising a program that includes business logic, because the logic already lives in its Ontology. That is also the limit: it works only inside Palantir, and the forward-deployed model it automates is the delivery model we would copy.
