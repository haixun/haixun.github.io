Summary

Amazon's cloud (public through Amazon.com). Bedrock offers supervised and reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT launched December 2025 for Nova and extended to open-weight models such as gpt-oss and Qwen in February 2026, with Lambda reward functions), custom-model import, and Nova Forge on SageMaker for building custom models from Nova checkpoints.

Our take

The hyperscaler path: every customisation knob exists, but the customer chooses the method and writes the reward. If a hyperscaler adds cross-approach profiling and calibration to Bedrock, that is the threat our strategy notes flag.

  • HQ: Seattle, WA
  • Pricing: Per training token or per hour; inference per token or provisioned throughput
