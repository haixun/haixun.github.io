Summary

Public data-cloud company (NYSE: SNOW). Cortex Training, announced at Summit in June and released to preview on 30 October 2026, is serverless SFT and RL (LoRA or full-parameter) of open-weight LLMs on data that stays in Snowflake, billed per second of GPU use in AI Credits; it succeeds Cortex Fine-tuning (LoRA only, GA February 2025).

Our take

Another platform moving up-stack into training for ML engineers, priced per unit of GPU time: a supplier and a data-gravity competitor, not a declare-and-optimise product. Sitting where the data already lives is the advantage we cannot match.
