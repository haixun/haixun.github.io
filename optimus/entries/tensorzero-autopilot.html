Summary

'An automated AI engineer' built on TensorZero's open-source LLMOps stack: it analysed observability data, created evaluations and LLM judges, optimised prompts and models and ran adaptive A/B tests. Offered only to a small group of companies with no published pricing; discontinued when the company wound down in June 2026.

Our take

The closest standalone attempt at an automated optimisation loop, killed by company failure eleven weeks after launch rather than by evidence that the loop does not work.

Key numbers

  • +3.4% to +612.7% over a GPT-5 mini baseline on eight tasks, limited to six comparably priced models, no fine-tuning (vendor-reported)
