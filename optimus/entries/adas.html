Summary

Defines automated design of agentic systems and presents Meta Agent Search, in which a meta agent iteratively programs new agents in code, drawing on an archive of prior discoveries. Because agents are code, the space covers prompts, tools, workflows and combinations.

Our take

The canonical statement of searching code space for agent designs; everything in the harness-search line descends from it. It optimises against a benchmark score and certifies nothing beyond it.

Key numbers

  • Discovered agents outperform hand-designed agents across coding, science and math, and keep their advantage when transferred across domains and models (author-reported)
