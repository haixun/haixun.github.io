People Prithvi Rajasekaran, Ethan Dixon, Carly Ryan, Jeremy Hadfield

Summary

Frames context engineering as the successor to prompt engineering: curating the smallest set of high-signal tokens given a finite attention budget and context rot. Covers compaction, structured note-taking, sub-agent architectures and just-in-time retrieval for long-horizon agents.

Our take

Context choices are part of the physical plan; this is the lab's manual playbook for a layer an optimiser should own.
