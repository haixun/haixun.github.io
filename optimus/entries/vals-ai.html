Summary

Independent evaluator of models and AI products for professional work: 38 benchmarks across legal, finance, tax, healthcare and more, each with a public validation set, a licensed private validation set and a private test set used for published results.

Our take

The best-funded independent evaluator, and evidence that standalone evaluation earns money by licensing private test sets to the vendors it evaluates. Shows why we should sell outcomes, not specifications or scores.
