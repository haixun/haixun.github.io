People Alexander Conway, Debadeepta Dey, Stefan Hackmann, Matthew Hausknecht, Michael Schmidt, Mark Steadman, Nick Volynets

Summary

Multi-objective Bayesian optimisation over a large space of agentic and non-agentic RAG configurations (vector stores, embedders, splitters, retrievers, synthesising LLMs, verifiers, rerankers) to find accuracy-cost Pareto frontiers, with early stopping to prune poor candidates. Open source, and later productised by DataRobot.

Our take

AutoML applied to RAG flows: a credible physical-plan search with a vendor behind it, but over a fixed template family rather than a customer's logical program, and certified only by benchmark score.

Key numbers

  • Flows about 9x cheaper on average while preserving most of the accuracy of the most accurate Pareto flows (author-reported)
