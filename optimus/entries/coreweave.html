Summary

Public GPU neocloud (NASDAQ: CRWV) that bought its way up-stack: Weights & Biases (completed May 2025), OpenPipe (announced September 2025) and the marimo notebook project. It launched Serverless RL (OpenPipe's ART framework and RULER verifier on a managed backend) in October 2025 and, on 30 September 2026, CoreWeave Forge, which unifies W&B Models, OpenPipe post-training and marimo into one run-observe-improve-evaluate loop; OpenPipe's hosted product was retired.

Our take

The compute cloud that most explicitly packages the improve-and-evaluate loop, but for ML engineers and to sell GPU time. Supplier and acquirer, not a neutral optimiser; its purchases of OpenPipe and W&B show where tooling startups in this space end up.

Key numbers

  • Serverless RL ~1.4x faster training and up to 40% lower cost than self-managed (vendor-reported)
  • B200 ~$8.60/GPU-hour list (vendor-reported)
  • HQ: Livingston, NJ
  • Pricing: GPU-hours; Forge in Free, Pro and Enterprise editions
