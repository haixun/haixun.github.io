The Shift from Models to Compound AI Systems

author-reported

structurephysical-planpromptsearchgeneralworkflow-searchcascades-routingprompt-optimisation

bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2024/02/18/compound-ai-systems

People Matei Zaharia, Omar Khattab, Lingjiao Chen, Jared Quincy Davis, Heather Miller, Chris Potts, James Zou, Michael Carbin, Jonathan Frankle, Naveen Rao, Ali Ghodsi

Summary

Argues state-of-the-art results increasingly come from systems of multiple model calls, retrievers and tools rather than single models, and that optimising such systems end to end is an open problem because components are non-differentiable. Points to DSPy and FrugalGPT as early optimisers.

Our take

The founding statement of 'the task is a program'; our contribution is to add business logic and a certificate to its optimisation agenda.
