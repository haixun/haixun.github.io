Summary

AI underwriting for insurance carriers (P&C and life and health), tuned per carrier on its underwriting manual and book. Ships an AI Accuracy Validator that scores output against what the carrier's underwriters would write, reruns after every code update and emits an audit-ready log.

Our take

The working comparable for selling the outcome and shipping the validator and compliance record as features; the per-customer contract accumulates as a by-product. The risk is a core-system vendor (Guidewire, an investor) bundling it.

Key numbers

  • 1.5M+ submissions across 50+ lines of business (vendor-reported)
  • Carriers representing about $265B-$270B in gross written premium (vendor-reported)
