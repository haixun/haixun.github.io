Summary

Compares MIPROv2, SIMBA and GEPA on Databricks' IE Bench information-extraction suite. GEPA-optimised gpt-oss-120b beats Claude Opus 4.1 and Sonnet 4 by about 3% at roughly 90x and 20x lower serving cost, and GEPA matches or beats supervised fine-tuning on a GPT-4.1 subset.

Our take

Strong vendor evidence that the physical-plan choice (small model plus optimised prompt) beats 'use the best model', and that prompt optimisation competes with fine-tuning; the optimiser itself is free.

Key numbers

  • GEPA-optimised gpt-oss-120b ~3% above Opus 4.1 at ~90x lower serving cost (vendor-reported)
  • SFT +1.9, GEPA +2.1, GEPA on SFT +4.8 on a GPT-4.1 subset (vendor-reported)
