Summary

Founded by the team behind the Falcon open models; sold Adaptive Engine, an 'RLOps' platform for enterprises to tune open models with RL and preference tuning from production feedback. Datadog acquired it on 30 June 2026 to join Datadog AI Research; terms undisclosed.

Our take

Enterprise RL-from-feedback as a standalone product ended inside an observability company, which suggests the trace data, not the trainer, is what buyers value.
