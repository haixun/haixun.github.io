Summary

Essay arguing that AI research is verifiable enough, through proxies like loss and efficiency, to be automated the way math was, compressing years of progress into one. Gives a 2028 median for AIs matching the best human AI researchers and a 25% chance it slips past 2032.

Our take

Its mechanism, that capability compounds where work is checkable, implies the bottleneck migrates to making enterprise work checkable, which is what a logical program plus contract does.

Key numbers

  • Median 2028 for AIs matching top human AI researchers; 25% chance after 2032 (estimate, author)
  • Claude 'leads' on 26%+ of Anthropic's AI R&D work in August, up from under 1% in February (press, cited by author)
