Summary

Successor to LAB-Bench with nearly 1,900 mostly open-response tasks measuring practical biology-research capabilities in more realistic settings, released with a public dataset and evaluation harness.

Our take

A domain benchmark built by an agent company to steer its own systems; a useful pattern for customer-specific acceptance sets.

Key numbers

  • Nearly 1,900 tasks (author-reported)
  • Model accuracy 26-46% lower than on LAB-Bench, depending on subtask (author-reported)
