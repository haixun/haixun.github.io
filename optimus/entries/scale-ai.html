Summary

The largest supplier of human-generated training and evaluation data, also selling an enterprise GenAI platform, evaluation services and government work. Meta bought a 49% non-voting stake for about $14.3B in June 2025 and founder Alexandr Wang left to run Meta's superintelligence group; some frontier-lab customers reportedly left.

Our take

A data and evaluation supplier, relevant as the incumbent for human-graded evals and as an alumni source for enterprise AI services teams (Delegate's founders). Not a competitor for program optimisation.
