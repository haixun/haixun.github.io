People Arjun Prakash, Derek Ho

Summary

Forward-deployed 'AI-native operations' company founded by two former Palantir staff, building and running production agentic systems for Fortune 500 firms in healthcare, financial services and telecom. Backers include Coatue, Lightspeed, Khosla, Microsoft and OpenAI.

Our take

Proof that enterprises pay well for delivered workflows rather than tools; it is the services shape we would start with. Its 'continuous improvement' is engineers, not an optimiser, which is exactly the labour an optimiser would compress.

Key numbers

  • $175M Series B at $1.8B post-money, September 2025 (press)
