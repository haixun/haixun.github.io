Summary

Began as programmatic data labelling (weak supervision, out of Stanford) sold as enterprise software, then repositioned as a data service selling finished datasets, RL environments and evaluation tasks to frontier labs, hyperscalers, enterprises and the US government. Raised a $350M Series E at $3.5B in September 2026.

Our take

Its valuation is the evidence that enterprises cannot specify what good looks like, which is the success-criteria problem a declare-and-optimise product inherits. It has moved toward selling to labs, so it is a data supplier and a sign of where criteria money goes, not a direct competitor.
