AFlow: Automating Agentic Workflow Generation

People Jiayi Zhang, Jinyu Xiang, Zhaoyang Yu, Fengwei Teng, Xiong-Hui Chen, Jiaqi Chen, Mingchen Zhuge, Xin Cheng, Sirui Hong, Jinlin Wang, Bingnan Zheng, Bang Liu, Yuyu Luo, Chenglin Wu

Summary

Reformulates workflow optimisation as Monte Carlo Tree Search over code-represented workflows of LLM-invoking nodes, refined through code modification, tree-structured experience and execution feedback, with dollar cost in the objective. Evaluated on six benchmarks.

Our take

The search-side workflow optimiser closest to a compiler's cost model, and the source of the graphs FlowCompile then configures. It still needs a reliable comparator and a benchmark-shaped metric.

Key numbers

  • 5.7% average improvement over state-of-the-art baselines across six benchmarks (author-reported)
  • Smaller models outperform GPT-4o on specific tasks at 4.55% of its dollar inference cost (author-reported)
