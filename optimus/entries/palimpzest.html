Summary

A declarative system for AI-powered analytical queries over unstructured data: users write the query, and a cost-based optimiser picks the physical plan (model, inference method, prompt strategy, parallelism) that best trades runtime, dollar cost and output quality. Evaluated on legal discovery, real-estate search and medical schema matching; Abacus is its later optimiser.

Our take

The logical-query/physical-plan separation applied to LLM pipelines, from the group whose senior author co-founded G5 Labs; quality is estimated rather than certified, and the logical layer is a data query, not business logic.

Key numbers

  • A plan 3.3x faster and 2.9x cheaper than the baseline with better quality (arXiv v2, author-reported)
  • With parallelism, up to 90.3x speedup at 9.1x lower cost than single-threaded GPT-4, with F1 within 83.5% of the baseline (arXiv v2, author-reported)
