People Chengrun Yang, Xuezhi Wang, Yifeng Lu, Hanxiao Liu, Quoc V. Le, Denny Zhou, Xinyun Chen

Summary

Optimization by PROmpting (OPRO) describes an optimisation task in natural language and lets an LLM propose new solutions from a prompt listing earlier solutions and their scores, iterating. Its main application is searching for instructions that maximise task accuracy.

Our take

Foundational for 'the LLM is the search operator'; single-prompt and metric-only, a baseline now rather than a method to build on.

Key numbers

  • Optimised prompts beat human-designed prompts by up to 8% on GSM8K and up to 50% on Big-Bench Hard tasks (author-reported)
