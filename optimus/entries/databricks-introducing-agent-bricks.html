Databricks: Introducing Agent Bricks

vendor-reported

promptweightsevaluatorsearchweight-updateestimatedocumentsenterprise-workflowprompt-optimisationfine-tuningcost-quality

databricks.com/blog/introducing-agent-bricks
press release: databricks.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/databricks-la

People Xiangrui Meng, Kasey Uhlenhuth, Hanlin Tang, Patrick Wendell, Matei Zaharia

Summary

Launch post for Agent Bricks (beta): describe a task in natural language, connect data, and the system generates task-specific evaluation data and LLM judges, then searches prompt engineering, fine-tuning, reward models and TAO to offer cost- or quality-optimised agents, steerable via expert feedback (ALHF).

Our take

The canonical 'declare and we optimise' pitch from an incumbent; worth rereading next to its later retirement into SQL functions and code-first agents.

Key numbers

  • 'Up to 10x lower cost' than prompt-optimised proprietary LLMs on a document-parsing benchmark (vendor-reported)
  • AstraZeneca parsed 400,000+ clinical-trial documents, agent built in under 60 minutes (customer-reported)
