Summary

Defines semantic operators (filter, join, group-by, top-k and aggregation with natural-language criteria) as a formalism whose reference semantics is a gold algorithm, and an optimiser that substitutes cheaper plans such as model cascades and embedding proxies while giving accuracy guarantees relative to that gold algorithm. Implemented in the open-source LOTUS system.

Our take

The semantic-operator system that states a guarantee, but relative to an expensive LLM 'gold algorithm', not to ground truth; the certificate we want is against the customer's labels and contract.

Key numbers

  • Semantic filter, join, group-by and top-k accelerated by up to 1,000x (author-reported)
  • Up to 170% higher quality than recent LLM analytic systems; up to 3.6x faster than the highest-quality baselines (author-reported)
