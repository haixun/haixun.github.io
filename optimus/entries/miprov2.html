Optimizing Instructions and Demonstrations for Multi-Stage Language Model Programs

arxiv.org/abs/2406.11695

People Krista Opsahl-Ong, Michael J Ryan, Josh Purtell, David Broman, Christopher Potts, Matei Zaharia, Omar Khattab

Summary

Introduces MIPRO, an optimiser that jointly tunes the free-form instructions and few-shot demonstrations of every module in a multi-stage LM program using only a downstream metric, with no module-level labels. It combines program- and data-aware instruction proposal, a surrogate model fitted on stochastic mini-batch evaluations, and meta-optimisation of the proposer; MIPROv2 ships in DSPy.

Our take

The workhorse baseline everything since compares against, and an honest treatment of credit assignment without step labels; still prompts only, against a metric the user must trust.

Key numbers

  • Outperforms baseline optimisers on 5 of 7 multi-stage programs with Llama-3-8B, by up to 13% accuracy (author-reported)
