Optimizing LLM Queries in Relational Data Analytics Workloads

author-reported

physical-planrewritedata-analyticscost-qualitysemantic-operators

arxiv.org/abs/2403.05821

People Shu Liu, Asim Biswal, Amog Kamsetty, Audrey Cheng, Luis Gaspar Schroeder, Liana Patel, Shiyi Cao, Xiangxi Mo, Ion Stoica, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Matei Zaharia

Summary

Reorders the rows and fields of an input table to maximise KV-cache reuse when LLMs are invoked over relational data, cutting cost and latency without changing the prompts' content. Applies to existing analytics engines and serving platforms.

Our take

One of the few answer-preserving rewrites in this space, worth having in any physical planner's toolbox.

Key numbers

  • Up to 3.4x faster job completion with Llama 3 models (author-reported)
  • 32% cost savings under OpenAI and Anthropic pricing (author-reported)
