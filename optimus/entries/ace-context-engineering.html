Summary

Treats contexts (system prompts, agent memory) as evolving playbooks updated through generation, reflection and curation, with incremental structured updates to avoid brevity bias and context collapse. Works offline and online, and can adapt from execution feedback without labels.

Our take

The baseline every harness paper compares against, and per AHE it regresses below seed when transferred while costing more tokens, since context interventions are paid at every call. Useful as the prompt-level reference point.

Key numbers

  • +10.6% on agent benchmarks and +8.6% on finance over strong baselines (author-reported)
  • Matches the top-ranked production agent on the AppWorld leaderboard average with a smaller open model (author-reported)
