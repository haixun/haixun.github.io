Summary

AI observability and evaluation platform (Arize AX and the source-available Phoenix project) that documented a full judge-to-human alignment workflow. Dynatrace agreed to acquire it for $915M on 13 August 2026; the deal closed 1 October 2026.

Our take

The one disclosed price in the eval layer, paid by a monitoring company: evaluation became a feature of observability. It tells us not to build an evaluation business and to price any exit as a platform tuck-in.

Key numbers

  • $70M Series C, February 2025, led by Adams Street Partners (vendor-reported)
