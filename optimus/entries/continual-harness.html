Summary

Grew out of Gemini Plays Pokémon: a reset-free harness for embodied agents that alternates between acting and refining its own prompt, sub-agents, skills and memory within a single run. It then adds an online process-reward co-learning loop where a frontier teacher relabels an open model's rollouts to update its weights.

Our take

Online, within-run adaptation without episode resets is the deployment shape enterprises actually have, but the evidence is a game and the acceptance logic is thin. Relevant as joint harness-and-weights co-optimisation.

Key numbers

  • Gemini Plays Pokémon, with human-in-the-loop harness refinement, completed Pokémon Blue, Yellow Legacy (hard mode) and Crystal without a lost battle (author-reported)
  • From scratch, recovers a majority of the gap to a hand-engineered expert harness on Pokémon Red and Emerald (author-reported)
