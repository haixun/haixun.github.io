ThetaEvolve: Test-time Learning on Open Problems

arxiv.org/abs/2511.23473
code: github.com/ypwang61/ThetaEvolve

People Yiping Wang, Shao-Rong Su, Zhiyuan Zeng, Eva Xu, Liliang Ren, Xinyu Yang

Summary

An open-source framework that simplifies and extends AlphaEvolve with a single LLM, a large program database and test-time reinforcement learning, so the model internalises evolving strategies. Lets an 8B open model reach new best-known bounds on two open problems from AlphaEvolve.

Our take

Program evolution plus weight updates in one loop, the same move SIA makes for harnesses; peripheral to enterprise workflows.

  • DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B reaches new best-known bounds on circle packing and the first auto-correlation inequality (author-reported)
