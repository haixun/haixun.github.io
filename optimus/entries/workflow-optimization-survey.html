Summary

Surveys workflow optimisation for LLM agents as agentic computation graphs, organised by when structure is determined (static vs dynamic), what part is optimised and which evaluation signal guides it. Proposes a ten-dimension minimum reporting protocol including seeds, repeated runs and structural variance, and catalogues 27 workflow-relevant evaluation assets.

Our take

Its reporting protocol is the published standard an acceptance gate would implement, and it lists a trustworthy evaluator as a precondition for static (compile-time) optimisation. Nobody yet runs it as a deployment gate.
