People Viraj Mehta, Gabriel Bianconi

Summary

Launch post for TensorZero Autopilot, which analyses observability data, builds evals and judges, optimises prompts and models and runs adaptive A/B tests. Reports gains on eight tasks against a GPT-5 mini baseline with five seeds, model choice limited to six comparably priced models and fine-tuning disabled.

Our take

The clearest public benchmark of a standalone automated optimisation loop; the spread (+3.4% to +613%) shows how task-dependent the gain is.

Key numbers

  • Data extraction 0.110 to 0.784 (+612.7%); retail customer service +3.4% (vendor-reported)
