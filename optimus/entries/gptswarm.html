Paper · 2024-02-26 · ICML 2024

Language Agents as Optimizable Graphs (GPTSwarm)

author-reported

promptstructuresearchmetricgeneralworkflow-searchprompt-optimisation

arxiv.org/abs/2402.16823
code: github.com/metauto-ai/gptswarm

People Mingchen Zhuge, Wenyi Wang, Louis Kirsch, Francesco Faccio, Dmitrii Khizbullin, Jürgen Schmidhuber

Summary

Describes LLM agents as computational graphs whose nodes are operations and edges information flow, composable into hierarchies of agents. Automatic optimisers refine node prompts and change edge connectivity to improve orchestration.

Our take

The first explicit 'workflow as optimisable graph' paper; largely superseded by AFlow and ADAS but the right ancestor for structure search.
