AutoMix: Automatically Mixing Language Models

Summary

Routes queries to larger models based on a small model's few-shot self-verification of its own answer, using a POMDP-based router to cope with noisy verification.

Our take

Self-verification as the cascade signal is cheap but noisy; invariants and checks in a business program are a better deferral signal where they exist.

Key numbers

  • Over 50% lower computational cost at comparable performance across five models and five datasets (author-reported)
