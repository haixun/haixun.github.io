CAESURA: Language Models as Multi-Modal Query Planners

author-reported

structurecompiledata-analyticssemantic-operatorsworkflow-compilation

arxiv.org/abs/2308.03424

People Matthias Urban, Carsten Binnig

Summary

Proposes language-model-driven query planning: an LLM translates natural-language queries into executable plans whose operators can process images, text and tables in data lakes. A GPT-4 prototype shows feasibility on two datasets.

Our take

An early statement of LLM-as-planner for multimodal data; a feasibility demo without cost or quality optimisation.
