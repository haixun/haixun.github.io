Summary

Evaluation and observability platform for LLM applications with a purpose-built trace store, open versioned judge (scorer) templates and seat-free pricing on data processed and scores computed.

Our take

The strongest independent left in a category being bought or bundled; useful as the eval substrate a customer may already have. It measures and does not optimise, and publishes no judge-human agreement.

Key numbers

  • $80M Series B led by ICONIQ, 17 February 2026 (vendor-reported)
  • 23.9x faster full-text search on 3.9M traces vs an unnamed competitor (vendor-reported)
  • $249/month professional tier; $4/GB and $2.50 per 1,000 scores on entry tier (vendor-reported)
