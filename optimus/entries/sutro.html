Summary

Managed, model-independent platform (Skysight, Inc.) that turns labelled examples into deployable 'AI Functions': evaluation, prompt and harness optimisation, model sweeps that show cost-quality trade-offs, and batch inference on open models, hosted by Sutro or in the customer's VPC. It positions itself explicitly as an alternative to fine-tuning.

Our take

Not cross-approach, but the small vendor closest to the document-processing problem shape and to optimising the physical plan against the customer's own labels; cheap and BYOC, with no attestations found. A direct comparable to watch.

  • HQ: San Francisco, CA
  • Customers: TravelJoy, Wellfound
  • Pricing: Starter $50/month (2 functions), Scale $500/month (25 functions), Enterprise custom; batch inference by usage.
