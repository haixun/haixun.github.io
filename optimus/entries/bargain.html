Summary

BARGAIN cascades a cheap LLM and an expensive 'oracle' LLM over records, choosing confidence thresholds with adaptive sampling and statistical estimation so that the output meets a target accuracy, precision or recall with stated confidence.

Our take

The closest thing in the literature to a per-step certificate with stated confidence; it certifies agreement with an oracle model on one operator, not end-to-end correctness against the customer's labels.

Key numbers

  • Reduces cost on average by up to 86% more than the state of the art across 8 real-world datasets (author-reported)
