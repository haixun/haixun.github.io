Summary

A 21-author review framing agent progress as externalisation: memory externalises state, skills externalise procedure, protocols externalise interaction structure, and harness engineering is the unification layer. It traces weights → context → harness and lists open challenges in evaluation and governance.

Our take

Names our category better than anyone: 'evaluation as externalized quality infrastructure rather than as a post-hoc measurement', and says benchmarks cannot attribute gains to the infrastructure. A written research agenda is also a sign the space may become free tooling.
