Summary

Throughput-first serverless inference for open models with per-request completion windows (asap, balanced, flex) priced below other providers, plus Sailboxes, persistent VMs for long-running agents. Serves uploaded LoRA adapters and RL rollouts and offers a drop-in Tinker integration; it does no training itself.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor: a cheap place to serve what a customisation layer produces, especially batch document work. LoRA serving was narrow when last checked (two base models, flex window only).
