People Walden Yan

Summary

Argues multi-agent designs are fragile because subagents act on unshared context and conflicting implicit decisions. Proposes two context-engineering principles (share full traces; actions carry implicit decisions) and defaults to single-threaded agents.

Our take

A practitioner case that structure choices (one agent vs many) are quality-critical and task-dependent, exactly the kind of decision structure search should make with evidence rather than doctrine.
