People Jenny Zhang, Bingchen Zhao, Wannan Yang, Jakob Foerster, Jeff Clune, Minqi Jiang, Sam Devlin, Tatiana Shavrina

Summary

Extends the Darwin Gödel Machine so that the task agent and the meta agent that modifies it live in one editable program, making the improvement procedure itself editable (DGM-H). Evaluated on coding, paper review, robotics reward design and Olympiad math grading, showing meta-level improvements that transfer across domains.

Our take

The research frontier of 'optimise the optimiser'. It removes the assumption that task skill and self-modification skill coincide, but says little about how a change is certified.

Key numbers

  • Outperforms baselines without self-improvement or open-ended exploration and prior DGM across four domains (author-reported)
