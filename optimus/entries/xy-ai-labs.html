Summary

Healthcare-operations AI company (revenue cycle: claims scrubbing, insurance verification, prior authorisation, coding) that also publishes research. Its 'Compiled AI' paper (arXiv 2604.05150, with Stanford Medicine, Cornell and Harvard co-authors) compiles LLM workflow steps into deterministic code via a 'Code Factory'.

Our take

The only company found pursuing the compile route (model steps become plain code) commercially, in a regulated domain. Small and early; worth watching as the closest product embodiment of compiling the physical plan.

Key numbers

  • $3M total, seed September 2025 (press, Caplight)
  • Compiled AI: 96% task completion on BFCL (n=400) with zero execution tokens; 57x token reduction at 1,000 transactions (author-reported)
