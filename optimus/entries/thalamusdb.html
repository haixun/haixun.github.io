Summary

An approximate query-processing engine for SQL extended with natural-language predicates over images, audio and text, evaluated by models. It uses cheap relational processing to limit model inference and returns deterministic bounds guaranteed to contain the full-data answer under its assumptions.

Our take

The database tradition's answer to 'how wrong could this be': bounds rather than point estimates, which is the shape a certificate should take.

Key numbers

  • 35.0x average speedup over MindsDB on Craigslist, YouTube and Netflix data (author-reported)
