Summary

A closed loop that evolves coding-agent harness components (tools, middleware, memory, system prompt) with three observability pillars: file-level components, a distilled evidence corpus from trajectories, and a self-declared prediction per edit checked against the next round. Ten iterations on Terminal-Bench 2 with GPT-5.4, then frozen and transferred to other models and to SWE-bench Verified.

Our take

The source of the half-life numbers in our argument: harnesses port across models but barely across tasks, and the loop is near-blind to its own regressions. The paper most invested in self-evolution supplies the best case for an independent acceptance gate.

Key numbers

  • Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 69.7% → 77.0% over ten iterations, above Codex at 71.9% (author-reported)
  • Frozen harness on SWE-bench Verified: 75.6% vs 75.2% seed (+0.4 pp over 500 tasks), regressing on 3 of 7 repositories; 12% fewer tokens (author-reported)
  • Cross-model on the same benchmark: +2.3 to +10.1 pp (author-reported)
  • Regression prediction precision 11.8% against a 5.6% baseline; fix prediction 33.7% against 6.5% (author-reported)
  • System prompt alone regresses (67.4% vs 69.7% seed); memory alone beats full AHE on the Hard tier (63.3% vs 53.3%) (author-reported)
