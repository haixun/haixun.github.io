People Prithvi Rajasekaran

Summary

Builds a planner-generator-evaluator harness for multi-hour autonomous app building, with an evaluator that tests the running app via Playwright against negotiated criteria. Shows components (context resets, sprints) becoming unnecessary as models improved, and recommends removing one component at a time to find what is load-bearing.

Our take

The best first-party evidence that harness components have a half-life tied to model generations, which is why the physical plan must be regenerated rather than owned.

Key numbers

  • Solo run 20 min, $9, broken result vs full harness 6 hr, $200 (vendor-reported)
  • V2 harness on Opus 4.6: 3 hr 50 min, $124.70 (vendor-reported)
