Summary

Open-source (Apache-2.0) library for fast, memory-efficient LoRA and full fine-tuning and RL of open models on modest GPUs, with Unsloth Studio, a no-code interface introduced in March 2026. YC-backed; no priced round beyond a small 2024 seed has been disclosed.

Our take

Makes the training step nearly free for anyone with an ML engineer, which reinforces that training is not where a new platform can win. A component an optimiser would call, not a competitor.
