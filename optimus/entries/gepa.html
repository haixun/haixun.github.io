Summary

A prompt optimiser that samples system trajectories (reasoning, tool calls, outputs), reflects on them in natural language to diagnose failures, proposes prompt updates, and keeps a Pareto frontier of candidates whose complementary lessons it merges. It targets any system with one or more LLM prompts and ships as dspy.GEPA and as a standalone library.

Our take

The strongest evidence that reading traces beats scalar reward for adapting programs cheaply, which is the sample-efficiency regime enterprises live in. It is still prompt-level search against a metric; reflection on traces is a component we would want, not a product.

Key numbers

  • Beats GRPO by 6% on average and up to 20% across six tasks with up to 35x fewer rollouts (author-reported)
  • Beats MIPROv2 by over 10%, e.g. +12% accuracy on AIME-2025 (author-reported)
