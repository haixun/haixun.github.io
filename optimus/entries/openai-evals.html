Summary

OpenAI's hosted evaluation dashboard, API and graders. Deprecation announced 3 June 2026; read-only from 31 October 2026 and shut down 30 November 2026, with users told to migrate to Promptfoo, which OpenAI acquired in March 2026. The open-source openai/evals repository is separate.

Our take

A frontier lab exiting hosted evaluation in favour of an acquired open-source tool: more evidence that evaluation alone is not a business, and that the labs will not own the customer's definition of correct.
