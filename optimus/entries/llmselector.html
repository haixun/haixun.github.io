Optimizing Model Selection for Compound AI Systems (LLMSelector)

arxiv.org/abs/2502.14815

People Lingjiao Chen, Jared Quincy Davis, Boris Hanin, Peter Bailis, Matei Zaharia, James Zou, Ion Stoica

Summary

Allocates a model to each module of a compound system (e.g. self-refine, multi-agent debate) by repeatedly picking a module and giving it the model with the best LLM-estimated module-wise performance, relying on the observation that end-to-end quality is often monotone in per-module quality. API calls scale linearly with the number of modules.

Our take

Per-step model choice with LLM-estimated step quality is the cheap version of our physical planner; its monotonicity assumption is what business logic (sums, thresholds) can break.

Key numbers

  • 5-70% accuracy gains over using the same LLM for all modules (author-reported)
