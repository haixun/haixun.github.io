Summary

Frontier model maker (Claude). It sells no fine-tuning on any model or platform, verified as a negative across its feature and pricing surface in November 2026; customisation is prompting, Agent Skills, Managed Agents and an 'Outcomes' primitive (target result plus rubric plus grader, iterated until met), with a self-serve BAA in the Console. It is also a backer of Ode, the services firm that acquired Fractional AI.

Our take

The only frontier lab a non-AI-native enterprise can use without an ML engineer, precisely because it sells no training; Outcomes is the closest existing interface primitive to declare-and-optimise, but only within the agentic approach. Through Ode it is also betting that this buyer needs consultants, so a product company may end up selling into its ecosystem rather than against it.
