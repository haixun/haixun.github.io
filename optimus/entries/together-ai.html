Summary

GPU and inference cloud for open models that launched Together Post-Training on 19 October 2026: customer-defined RL and SFT loops (LoRA, full-weight on some models, GRPO and custom losses, asynchronous RL) billed per GPU-minute on dedicated resources, alongside its older job-based fine-tuning service.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor; economics favour training tokens and GPU-minutes. Its advice on when not to train is editorial, and its real 'search' across approaches is a forward-deployed engineer.
