SIA: Self Improving AI with Harness & Weight Updates

People Prannay Hebbar, Yogendra Manawat, Samuel Verboomen, Alesia Ivanova, Selvam Palanimalai, Kunal Bhatia, Vignesh Baskaran

Summary

A Feedback-Agent that, given a task specification and verifier, updates both the scaffold and the weights of a task-specific agent, joining the harness-update and test-time-training lines. Evaluated on Chinese legal charge classification (LawBench), GPU kernel optimisation and single-cell RNA denoising.

Our take

A startup's argument that the optimiser should choose between harness and weights per task, which is the physical-plan view applied to self-improvement. Gains are against prior SOTA on three narrow benchmarks.

Key numbers

  • 25.1% over prior SOTA on LawBench (author-reported)
  • GPU kernels 12.4% faster than prior SOTA (1,017 vs 1,161 µs) (author-reported)
  • 20.4% over prior SOTA on single-cell denoising (author-reported)
