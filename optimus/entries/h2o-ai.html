Summary

Enterprise AutoML and generative-AI platform (Driverless AI, h2oGPTe, open-source H2O-3 and LLM Studio). It deploys on-prem and air-gapped with HIPAA and FedRAMP High posture; its last disclosed round is a $100M Series E in November 2021 at $1.7B, led by Commonwealth Bank of Australia.

Our take

Proof that AutoML, the previous generation of 'declare a target, we search the approach', became a compliance-heavy enterprise niche rather than a category winner. Strong deployment posture, little momentum.
