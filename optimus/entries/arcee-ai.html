Summary

Started selling small-language-model customisation (model merging via MergeKit, continual pretraining, distillation) to enterprises, then became an open-weight model lab with the Apache-licensed Trinity family, including the 400B Trinity Large. Raised a Series B of at least $150M at a $1B pre-money in September 2026.

Our take

A customisation specialist that concluded the money was in owning base models, not tuning customers' ones: another exit from the middle layer.
