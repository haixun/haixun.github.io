Summary

A frontier lab founded by ex-Anthropic researchers whose product is an automated AI R&D loop: its own base models (Mirendil-Base, Fern) plus Lor, a free private-beta agent platform that runs hundreds of long-lived agents with compute included. It reports one researcher with agents pretraining a 100B–500B-parameter coding model in 3.5 months for under $10M.

Our take

The strongest existence proof that the engineering loop automates, and its own account says the judgment about what to try and what to trust stayed human, which is the measurement thesis. Vertically integrated (own models, own platform, Google Cloud), the opposite of a neutral optimiser, and it names a hospital training on its own data as a target customer.

Key numbers

  • Mirendil-Base pretrained in 3.5 months for under $10M all-in; agents wrote >99% of the code (vendor-reported)
  • Fern 0.1 scores 6.1 on Terminal Bench 4.0 vs 66.4 for Claude Opus 5.5 (vendor-reported)
