Summary

An 'execution layer for agentic inference' that schedules each job onto the cheapest compute meeting its deadline across NVIDIA, AMD, TPU and AWS accelerators, with realtime, async (Standard) and batch modes. Sold partly through AWS Marketplace, with private deployments for data residency.

Our take

A scheduler at the serving layer, not an optimiser of the program; opaque (no founders, funding or attestations disclosed) and no public LoRA serving.
