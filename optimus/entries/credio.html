Summary

Builds 'AI teammates' for merchant risk operations (merchant underwriting, fraud review, KYB and sponsor-bank compliance) that review cases inside a team's existing tools and produce explainable recommendations analysts approve, override or refine. Positions itself as 'decision intelligence for merchant risk operations'.

Our take

A vertical decision workflow in a regulated domain where SOPs are the business logic and analyst overrides are free labels: the shape of customer we would serve, or a company that could grow into our loop for one vertical.
