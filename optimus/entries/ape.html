Summary

Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) treats the instruction as a program: an LLM proposes a pool of candidate instructions, each is scored by another model's zero-shot performance, and the best is selected. It established automatic instruction search as a line of work.

Our take

The origin point of prompt search; historically important, practically superseded.

Key numbers

  • Better than or comparable to human-written instructions on 19 of 24 NLP tasks (author-reported)
