Abacus: A Cost-Based Optimizer for Semantic Operator Systems

author-reported

arxiv.org/abs/2505.14661

People Matthew Russo, Chunwei Liu, Sivaprasad Sudhir, Gerardo Vitagliano, Michael Cafarella, Tim Kraska, Samuel Madden

Summary

An extensible cost-based optimizer inside Palimpzest that chooses physical implementations for semantic operators to optimise quality, cost or latency subject to constraints on the others. It estimates operator quality from a few validation examples, priors or a model judge, and treats operator search as an infinite-armed bandit.

Our take

The closest published thing to the physical-plan optimiser in owning-the-intelligence, built by G5 Labs' chief executive's group. It optimises a fixed pipeline the user writes, estimates quality per operator rather than through the program, and gives no certificate.

Key numbers

  • 6.7–39.4% better quality, 10.8× cheaper and 3.4× faster than the next-best system on BioDEX, CUAD and MMQA (v3, author-reported)
