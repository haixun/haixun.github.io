Summary

Document-processing cloud for extraction, classification and splitting with LLMs. Its Composer agent (September 2025) tunes extraction schemas against the customer's eval set, field by field.

Our take

A working example of 'declare a schema, supply ground truth, we optimise' that survives because the scope is narrow and the metric (field accuracy) is unambiguous. Nothing in it handles business logic across steps.
