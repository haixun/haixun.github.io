Hybrid LLM: Cost-Efficient and Quality-Aware Query Routing

People Dujian Ding, Ankur Mallick, Chi Wang, Robert Sim, Subhabrata Mukherjee, Victor Ruhle, Laks V. S. Lakshmanan, Ahmed Hassan Awadallah

Summary

A router predicts query difficulty and sends each query to a small (edge) or a large (cloud) model, with a desired quality level tunable at test time to trade quality for cost.

Our take

A clean two-model router with a dial; one of many interchangeable routing operators.

Key numbers

  • Up to 40% fewer calls to the large model with no drop in response quality (author-reported)
