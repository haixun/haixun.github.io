Self-Harness: Harnesses That Improve Themselves

author-reported

harness-codesearchmetriccodegeneralharness-engineeringself-improvement

arxiv.org/abs/2606.09498

People Hangfan Zhang, Shao Zhang, Kangcong Li, Chen Zhang, Yang Chen, Yiqun Zhang, Lei Bai, Shuyue Hu

Summary

An agent improves its own harness without a stronger external agent, in a loop of weakness mining from traces, minimal harness proposals, and validation by regression test. A candidate is promoted if held-in improves without measurable held-out degradation, judged on two repeated attempts. Run on Terminal-Bench-2.0, SWE-bench Verified and AppWorld with MiniMax M2.5, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B and GLM-5.

Our take

The acceptance rule is explicit, which is useful, and thin (two attempts), which is the point. Model-specific harness tuning is the half-life problem in miniature.

Key numbers

  • Every final harness improves held-in and held-out pass rates across all nine model–benchmark pairs (author-reported)
  • Relative gains up to 132% (author-reported)
