Summary

Enterprise AutoML company that moved into generative and agentic AI. Its research team, including CTO Michael Schmidt, published syftr (AutoML 2025), a multi-objective Bayesian search over about 10^23 retrieval and agentic workflow configurations, and productised it as 'Generative Syftr search' in its SDK and generative-AI playground in 2026.

Our take

The counter-example to 'declare-and-optimise always fails': the search survived as a feature inside a platform that already holds the data and runtime. It searches configurations of a RAG or agent flow, not a customer's business logic, and certifies only against the user's eval set.
