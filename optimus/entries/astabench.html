AstaBench: Rigorous Benchmarking of AI Agents with a Scientific Research Suite

author-reported

metricscienceevaluationcost-quality

arxiv.org/abs/2510.21652
code: github.com/allenai/asta-bench

People Jonathan Bragg, Mike D'Arcy, Doug Downey, Yoav Goldberg, Ashish Sabharwal, Daniel S. Weld, et al.

Summary

A suite of 2,400+ problems spanning the scientific discovery process, with a reproducible research environment of production-grade search tools, standard agent interfaces, and scoring that accounts for cost and tool access. Ships nine classes of science-optimised Asta agents and many baselines.

Our take

Its insistence on reporting cost and controlling tool access alongside accuracy is the reporting discipline an optimiser's certificate needs.

  • 2,400+ problems; 57 agents across 22 agent classes evaluated (author-reported)
