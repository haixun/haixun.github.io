Summary

A case study of four end-to-end attempts to generate ML research papers autonomously with a six-agent pipeline and minimal scaffolding. Three failed during implementation or evaluation; one was accepted at Agents4Science 2025. Documents six recurring failure modes.

Our take

The corpus's only negative-results paper. The failure mode that matters to a buyer is 'overexcitement that declares success despite obvious failures': the system reports success rather than failing loudly, which is why the evaluator must sit outside it.
