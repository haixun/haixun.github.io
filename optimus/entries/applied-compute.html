Summary

Founded by former OpenAI researchers; builds custom models and agents for enterprises and runs AC2 (Agent Cloud: training, inference and continual improvement on one platform) plus AC Inference. Engagements supply researchers who sit with the customer's engineers or manage training and serving for them; no prices are published. Acquired the AI-services startup Delegate on 12 November 2026.

Our take

A services business wearing a platform's clothes, and the best-capitalised proof that the post-training buyer wants people as much as product. It sells weights to AI-native firms with ML teams; it is not building a declare-and-optimise layer, but it would be the obvious acquirer or competitor if one worked.

Key numbers

  • $100M annualised run rate in under 18 months (vendor-reported; no Form D on EDGAR)
  • AC Inference serves over 1 trillion tokens a day (vendor-reported)
  • Over 1,700 training runs on AC2 since July 2026; 40% of customers use both training and inference (vendor-reported)
