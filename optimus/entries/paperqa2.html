Summary

PaperQA2 is a literature-research agent optimised for factuality and compared against subject-matter experts with unrestricted tools on retrieval, summarisation and contradiction detection. The paper also introduces LitQA2, the benchmark that guided its design.

Our take

A harness tuned against a purpose-built benchmark and then audited by experts; the human-comparison protocol is more useful to us than the agent.

Key numbers

  • Matches or exceeds subject-matter experts on three literature research tasks (author-reported)
  • 2.34 +/- 1.99 contradictions identified per biology paper, 70% validated by experts (author-reported)
