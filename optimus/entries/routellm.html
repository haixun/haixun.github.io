Paper · 2024-06-26 · UC Berkeley (LMSYS), Anyscale · ICLR 2025

RouteLLM: Learning to Route LLMs with Preference Data

author-reported

physical-planweight-updatemetricgeneralcascades-routingcost-quality

arxiv.org/abs/2406.18665
code: github.com/lm-sys/RouteLLM

People Isaac Ong, Amjad Almahairi, Vincent Wu, Wei-Lin Chiang, Tianhao Wu, Joseph E. Gonzalez, M Waleed Kadous, Ion Stoica

Summary

Trains routers that send each query to a strong or a weak LLM, using human preference data plus augmentation. The routers keep their performance when the strong and weak models are swapped at test time.

Our take

A practical open-source router; preference-trained routing suits chat, less so business programs where correctness is checkable per step.

Key numbers

  • Cost reduced by over 2x in some cases without lowering response quality (author-reported)
