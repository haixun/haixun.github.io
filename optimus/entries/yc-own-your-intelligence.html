Summary

YC's Winter 2027 request for startups that help companies own specialised models tuned on their data instead of renting frontier intelligence, noting open-weight models are now competitive. Says someone must turn workflows into evals, expertise into training data and failures into improvements, and carry that to each new base model; strongest case in regulated industries.

Our take

Names the right buyer and the right labour, but frames the asset as the model. We argue the asset is the logical program and contract; the model is one physical choice the optimiser makes.
