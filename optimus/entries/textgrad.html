Paper · 2024-06-11 · Stanford · Nature, 2025

TextGrad: Automatic "Differentiation" via Text

author-reported

promptharness-codecredit-assignmentmetricgeneralscienceprompt-optimisation

arxiv.org/abs/2406.07496
code: github.com/zou-group/textgrad

People Mert Yuksekgonul, Federico Bianchi, Joseph Boen, Sheng Liu, Zhi Huang, Carlos Guestrin, James Zou

Summary

A PyTorch-style framework that backpropagates natural-language feedback from LLMs through a computation graph of compound-system components (prompts, code, molecules, treatment plans) and uses it to update each variable. Users supply only the objective.

Our take

Names the credit-assignment problem for programs of model calls and gives a usable mechanism; its 'gradients' are unverified LLM opinions, which is why invariants as free intermediate labels matter more for business programs.

Key numbers

  • GPT-4o zero-shot accuracy on Google-Proof QA from 51% to 55% (author-reported)
  • 20% relative gain on LeetCode-Hard solutions (author-reported)
