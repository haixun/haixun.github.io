EvoPrompt: Connecting LLMs with Evolutionary Algorithms Yields Powerful Prompt Optimizers

author-reported

promptsearchmetricgeneralprompt-optimisation

arxiv.org/abs/2309.08532

People Qingyan Guo, Rui Wang, Junliang Guo, Bei Li, Kaitao Song, Xu Tan, Guoqing Liu, Jiang Bian, Yujiu Yang

Summary

Uses an LLM to implement evolutionary operators (genetic algorithm and differential evolution) over a population of natural-language prompts, selecting on a development set. Evaluated on 31 datasets with GPT-3.5 and Alpaca.

Our take

Population-based prompt search, an ancestor of GEPA's Pareto evolution; a baseline, with no new idea for program-level optimisation.

Key numbers

  • Up to 25% better than human-engineered prompts and prior automatic methods on BBH (author-reported)
