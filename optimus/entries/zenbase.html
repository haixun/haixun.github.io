Summary

YC S24 company founded by two DSPy contributors that shipped an MIT-licensed library applying DSPy optimisers to existing code plus a hosted API that learned from feedback. The library was last updated in April 2025, and by October 2025 the company had become The Synthesis Company, selling systematic literature reviews.

Our take

A pivot by a two-person team a year out of YC: too small a sample to say the 'optimise your prompts as a service' market is dead, but consistent with the per-call optimiser being free.
