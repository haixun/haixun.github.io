Meta-Harness: End-to-End Optimization of Model Harnesses

People Yoonho Lee, Roshen Nair, Qizheng Zhang, Kangwook Lee, Omar Khattab, Chelsea Finn

Summary

An outer-loop search over harness code in which an agentic proposer reads the source, scores and execution traces of every prior candidate through a filesystem rather than compressed summaries. There is no per-candidate acceptance gate: all candidates are kept and a Pareto frontier is selected at the end. Evaluated on online text classification, retrieval-augmented math and TerminalBench-2.

Our take

Strong evidence that rich trace access beats scalar-feedback optimisers, from DSPy's author among others. Its lack of any acceptance gate is the gap an independent evaluator fills.

Key numbers

  • +7.7 points over ACE on online text classification with 4× fewer context tokens (author-reported)
  • +4.7 points average on 200 IMO-level problems across five held-out models with one discovered harness (author-reported)
  • 37.6% on TerminalBench-2 with Claude Haiku 4.5, above all reported hand-written Haiku harnesses (author-reported)
