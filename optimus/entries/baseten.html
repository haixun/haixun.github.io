Summary

Dedicated inference platform that has moved into training: Baseten Training and the Loops SDK (early access from May 2026, Tinker-compatible, SFT and asynchronous RL on Qwen and Kimi families), later joined by Serverless Loops priced per token. Offers a workload plane that runs in the customer's VPC.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor; economics favour training and dedicated GPU-minutes. Strongest deployment-in-customer-VPC story among the clouds, which matters more to regulated buyers than any optimiser does.
