Summary

Visual, node-based agent-workflow builder in OpenAI's AgentKit. Deprecation announced 3 June 2026; scheduled to shut down 30 November 2026, with users directed to the Agents SDK or ChatGPT Workspace Agents (ChatKit remains).

Our take

A frontier lab retreating from no-code workflow authoring: the logical program belongs in code or in the customer's own platform, not in a lab's canvas.
