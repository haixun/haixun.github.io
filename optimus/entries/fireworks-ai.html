Summary

Inference cloud for open models that also sells managed fine-tuning (SFT, DPO, ORPO), a Training API for customer-written loops including RL, reinforcement fine-tuning against customer-written evaluators, and a researcher-led services arm (Fireworks Lab). Fine-tuned models are served at base-model prices, so training pulls inference volume onto Fireworks.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor: it sells the training-and-deployment half of the thesis but never chooses the approach, and its economics favour training on Fireworks. Approach choice is sold as people (Fireworks Lab, 6-10 weeks), which is either evidence it is hard to automate or its obvious next product.

Key numbers

  • $1.505B Series D at $17.5B valuation, July 2026 (press)
  • Annualised run rate above $1B, 5x year on year (vendor-reported)
  • Harvey: 19.7% full-pass on its legal benchmark vs 11.5% for a frontier model at about a third of the cost per task (vendor-reported)
  • HIPAA with BAA, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001/27701/42001, zero data retention by default (vendor-reported)
