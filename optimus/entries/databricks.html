Summary

Data and AI platform (Mosaic AI). Its Agent Bricks: Custom LLM took a task description and data, refined prompts, inferred evaluation guidelines, compared prompt optimisation with fine-tuning and TAO, and deployed the winner; its documentation now reads 'This is a legacy offering' and points to AI Functions such as ai_query, with no announcement explaining the retirement. Knowledge Assistant is priced per answer.

Our take

The best-capitalised incumbent shipped declare-and-optimise and quietly retired it, which makes it the most important graveyard entry. It also never ran on regulated data: the Enhanced Security and Compliance workspace needed for PCI and HIPAA was not supported by Custom LLM.
