Summary

Alphabet's model lab (Gemini), sold to enterprises through Google Cloud's Vertex AI. Gemini fine-tuning ended on the Gemini API in 2025 and survives as supervised tuning on Vertex AI, which by user reports had not reached the Gemini 3.x models in late 2026. Google invested in Mirendil's Series A and supplies its compute.

Our take

Like OpenAI, Google is de-emphasising self-serve tuning of its frontier models, leaving customisation to open weights and the clouds. Its research line (AlphaEvolve) matters more to us than its tuning product.
