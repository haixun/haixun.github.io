Summary

European vendor (Invergent S.A.) selling agents, workflow processes and fine-tuned models that run in the customer's cloud, on its own servers, air-gapped, or in Surogate's EU cloud; products include the Surogate 3.7 Flash model, Train, Keel, a gateway and a desktop app. No disclosed funding, named customers or attestations.

Our take

A sovereignty-first bundle of model plus agents for regulated European buyers; relevant as a deployment-model comparator, not as an optimiser.

  • HQ: Bucharest, Romania
  • Pricing: Fixed cost on customer-owned hardware; EU API from $0.15 per million input tokens; resellers earn 30–40% (vendor-reported)
