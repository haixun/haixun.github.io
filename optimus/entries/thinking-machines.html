People Mira Murati

Summary

Model lab founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati. Its product Tinker is a low-level training API: the customer's code controls the loop, data and rewards for SFT, RL and distillation on open-weight models while Tinker runs the GPUs, priced per million training, sampling and prefill tokens; it also serves its own Inkling models. Trajectory runs on Tinker and is listed on its page as a user.

Our take

A supplier, not a competitor: the substrate that post-training startups like Trajectory build on. Weak enterprise posture (SOC 2 Type I only, no HIPAA, per our research), and its next round, $5B at a $40B pre-money, is reported as near close but unconfirmed after an earlier attempt at $50–60B collapsed.
