Summary

Public data-security company (NYSE: RBRK) that acquired Predibase, a LoRA fine-tuning and serving startup, in 2025. Its 10-Q puts the price at $109.1M, of which $92.9M was goodwill attributed primarily to the assembled workforce and only $11.0M developed technology on a two-year life; predibase.com now redirects to Rubrik Agent Cloud, which carries no fine-tuning content.

Our take

Not a player in optimisation; it is in the catalog as the buyer whose filing shows what a fine-tuning platform was worth on exit, which was mostly the team. The category produced acqui-hires.
