Summary

Account of an OpenAI team that built and shipped an internal product with Codex writing all the code; engineers designed the environment, constraints, documentation and feedback loops instead of writing code. Names the practice 'harness engineering'.

Our take

Popularised the term and shows the harness, not the model, as the engineered artefact; it is all manual, which is the labour an optimiser would target.

Key numbers

  • About a million lines of code and about 1,500 merged PRs over five months, three engineers driving Codex at the start (vendor-reported)
