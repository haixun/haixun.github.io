Summary

Public company (NASDAQ: MSFT); Microsoft Foundry is its model and agent platform. It sells supervised and reinforcement fine-tuning of OpenAI models (GPT-5 RFT moved to gated GA in May 2026), introduced 'Frontier Tuning' with managed RL environments at Build 2026, and put an Agent Optimizer, a closed loop for hosted agents, into private preview in mid-2026.

Our take

Notable because Foundry keeps selling OpenAI-model fine-tuning and is adding agent optimisation while OpenAI's own platform retreats. A hyperscaler shipping a closed-loop agent optimiser is the closest big-company move toward our thesis; watch whether it certifies anything.

Key numbers

  • Frontier Tuning: tuned models match GPT-5.4 at one-tenth the cost (vendor-reported)
  • HQ: Redmond, WA
  • Pricing: Per training token or per hour; per-token inference
