Summary

Evaluation company with the strongest published research in the layer (Lynx hallucination detector, Glider judge, FinanceBench, TRAIL). In 2026 it repositioned as a behavioural research lab building RL environments and world models, withdrew public pricing and retired its product SDKs.

Our take

The party with the best information left the evaluation-product business, which is the strongest market signal against building one. Its pivot to RL environments shows where evaluation expertise is now paid for: training data for labs.

Key numbers

  • TRAIL: best model localised errors 11% of the time on 148 traces, 841 errors (author-reported)
