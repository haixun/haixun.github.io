Kosmos: An AI Scientist for Autonomous Discovery

author-reported

arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824

People Ludovico Mitchener, Angela Yiu, Benjamin Chang, Michael Skarlinski, Samuel G. Rodriques, Michaela M. Hinks, Andrew D. White, et al.

Summary

An AI scientist that runs up to 12 hours of parallel data-analysis and literature-search cycles coordinated through a structured world model, and writes reports in which every statement is cited to code or literature. Seven highlighted discoveries span metabolomics, materials science, neuroscience and statistical genetics.

Our take

A long-horizon harness whose coherence comes from explicit shared state (the world model) rather than a longer context, with traceable outputs; it is audited by people, not optimised.

Key numbers

  • About 200 agent rollouts, 42,000 lines of code and 1,500 papers read per run on average (author-reported)
  • 79.4% of report statements judged accurate by independent scientists (author-reported)
  • A 20-cycle run equalled about 6 months of collaborators' own research time on average (author-reported)
