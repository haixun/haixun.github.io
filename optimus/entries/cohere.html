Summary

Enterprise model company selling bespoke custom-model development, under a BAA where needed, deployed in the customer's own private environment including air-gapped; customisation is 'contact sales' with no self-serve path. Roughly 85% of revenue comes from private deployments; it agreed to merge with Aleph Alpha in April 2026 and announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to C$7B at C$35.7B on 29 October 2026.

Our take

The clearest existence proof that non-AI-native enterprises can be sold customisation, by people plus private deployment rather than product. The bar for any optimiser selling to the same buyer: run where Cohere runs.
