Summary

Fine-tuning and serving platform for open models built around Ludwig and LoRAX (many LoRA adapters served on one GPU). Rubrik announced the acquisition on 25 June 2025; predibase.com now redirects to Rubrik Agent Cloud, which carries no fine-tuning content.

Our take

The clearest exit price in the category: Rubrik's 10-Q puts the deal at $109.1M with 85% goodwill attributed to the assembled workforce and $11.0M of technology on a two-year life. Fine-tuning tooling alone was valued as an acqui-hire.

Key numbers

  • Purchase price $109.1M, of which $92.9M goodwill 'primarily attributed to assembled workforce' (Rubrik 10-Q, independent)
  • Developed technology $11.0M on a two-year useful life; $40.4M three-year retention stock (Rubrik 10-Q, independent)
