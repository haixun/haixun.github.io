Summary

Serverless GPU cloud billed per second that added training products in October 2026: Modal Dojo, a hands-off library where the customer supplies model and data, and Spindle, an open-source Tinker-compatible training server running in the customer's Modal workspace (multi-tenant LoRA and single-tenant full-parameter). Also sells sandboxes and multi-node clusters.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor: the most flexible substrate for running whatever an optimiser chooses, but it sells GPU-seconds and has no reason to say 'don't train'. BAA is Enterprise-only and there is no customer-VPC option.
