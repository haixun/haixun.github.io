Summary

Publicly listed (Nasdaq: NBIS) full-stack AI cloud. Its Token Factory, launched November 2025 as the successor to AI Studio, bundles open-model inference with LoRA and full fine-tuning and one-click deployment; it acquired the inference-optimisation company Eigen AI in May 2026.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor: cheap reserved GPUs plus a token platform. Buying Eigen AI shows the clouds absorbing inference optimisation as a feature.
