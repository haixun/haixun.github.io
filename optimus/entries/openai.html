Summary

Frontier model maker and the largest API vendor. In 2026 it began retiring its customisation and evaluation surface: self-serve fine-tuning closed to organisations that had never run it on 7 May 2026 and to inactive ones on 2 July 2026, and active customers lose new job creation on 6 January 2027; the Evals dashboard, API and graders and Agent Builder shut down on 30 November 2026, with the Agents SDK, ChatGPT Workspace Agents and Promptfoo named as replacements.

Our take

The clearest retreat in the space: the lab with the most enterprise distribution decided sold fine-tuning and a hosted eval and agent builder were not worth running. It removes a would-be competitor and pushes customers who still need customisation onto open models and the compute clouds, which is where an optimiser that owns the program would sit.
