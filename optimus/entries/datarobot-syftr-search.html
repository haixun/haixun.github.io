Summary

SDK and playground feature that runs a syftr study over a configurable space of LLM blueprints against a grounding dataset and an evaluation dataset, with chosen objectives, a trial budget, optional max_cost and Pareto pruning, and returns the Pareto front. Added to DataRobot's SDK as SearchStudy in May 2026; docs updated 7 November 2026.

Our take

Live proof that workflow-configuration search can ship as a platform feature. It optimises RAG and agent configurations against the user's eval set; it neither reads business logic nor certifies the result.
