People Kyle Corbitt, David Corbitt

Summary

Started as a fine-tuning service that distilled frontier-model prompts into cheaper fine-tuned models from logged traffic, then built ART (Agent Reinforcement Trainer), an open-source library for training agents with RL. CoreWeave agreed to acquire it on 3 September 2025; the site now says the platform has migrated to Weights & Biases and CoreWeave, and the hosted product is gone.

Our take

The 'distil your production prompts into a small model' pitch is the weight-update rung of our ladder sold as a product, and it ended inside a GPU cloud, which is where that economics belongs. ART remains a usable open component.
