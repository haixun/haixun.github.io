People Shangyin Tan, Lakshya A Agrawal, Arnav Singhvi, Liheng Lai, Michael J Ryan, Dan Klein, Omar Khattab, Koushik Sen, Matei Zaharia

Summary

A benchmark of over 2,000 combinations of tasks, program architectures, optimisers and LMs for studying the quality-cost trade-offs of optimised language programs.

Our take

The one benchmark that treats program architecture and optimiser as variables; its finding that human judgement is still needed to pick compositions is the gap a program optimiser would fill.
