Automatic Prompt Optimization with "Gradient Descent" and Beam Search (ProTeGi)

author-reported

promptcredit-assignmentsearchmetricgeneralprompt-optimisation

arxiv.org/abs/2305.03495

People Reid Pryzant, Dan Iter, Jerry Li, Yin Tat Lee, Chenguang Zhu, Michael Zeng

Summary

Uses minibatches of data to produce natural-language 'gradients' criticising a prompt, then edits the prompt in the opposite semantic direction, with beam search and bandit selection for efficiency. Evaluated on three NLP tasks and LLM jailbreak detection.

Our take

The origin of textual-gradient prompt optimisation that TextGrad later generalised to programs; prompt-level only.

Key numbers

  • Improves an initial prompt's performance by up to 31% (author-reported)
