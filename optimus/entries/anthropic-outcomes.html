Summary

Beta feature of Claude Managed Agents: the user gives a description and a required markdown rubric, and the harness provisions a grader in a separate context window that scores the artifact per criterion and feeds failures back to the agent, iterating until satisfied or max_iterations (default 3, max 20). Not eligible for zero data retention (except self-hosted) or HIPAA.

Our take

A frontier lab productising the evaluator-outside-the-loop pattern, per artifact and per session. It certifies one output against a rubric, not a program under cost and error constraints, and its data-handling limits keep it out of the regulated work we target.
