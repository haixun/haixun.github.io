Summary

Commercial spin-out of the non-profit FutureHouse that sells Kosmos, an AI scientist running 12-hour sessions over literature and data, alongside the PaperQA2 literature agent lineage. Deals with Incyte (May 2026) and Pfizer (announced 29 October 2026), delivered with forward-deployed scientists and engineers.

Our take

A science-domain example of selling an outcome-producing workflow with forward-deployed people; its own evaluation shows interpretation lagging reading by over 20 points, the gap a certificate would have to address.

Key numbers

  • $70M seed at about $250M valuation, December 2025 (press)
  • Kosmos 79.4% statement accuracy (85.5% data, 82.1% literature, 57.9% interpretation) (author-reported)
