Summary

An open-source LLM-driven program-evolution framework aimed at sample efficiency, with balanced parent sampling, code-novelty rejection sampling and bandit-based selection over an LLM ensemble. Applied to circle packing, agentic harnesses for AIME, ALE-Bench and mixture-of-experts load-balancing losses.

Our take

An open, cheaper AlphaEvolve; relevant because sample efficiency is the binding cost of any search-route optimiser, and it explicitly designs agent harnesses.
