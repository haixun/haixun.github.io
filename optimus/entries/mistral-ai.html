Summary

European open-weight model maker. Forge, introduced 17 March 2026, is a system for enterprises to build models grounded in proprietary knowledge, delivered with forward-deployed scientists and on-prem options; it also sells API fine-tuning. It raised a €3B Series D at a post-money valuation above €21B on 8 September 2026.

Our take

The European analogue of Cohere's play: customisation sold as a forward-deployed service plus private deployment, the opposite direction to OpenAI and Anthropic, which are leaving sold customisation.
