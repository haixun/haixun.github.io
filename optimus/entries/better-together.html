Summary

BetterTogether alternates prompt optimisation and fine-tuning of the module LMs in a modular pipeline so the same model teaches itself, without intermediate labels. Released as a DSPy optimiser.

Our take

Evidence that the prompt and weight rungs compound; relevant when a customer's physical plan includes small models it owns.

Key numbers

  • Beats optimising weights alone by up to 60% and prompts alone by up to 6%, on average across LMs and tasks (author-reported)
