Summary

Compute and post-training company with an open-source bent: Model Factory (SFT and RL on open models via its open-source prime-rl trainer), serverless and dedicated inference, GPU compute, agent sandboxes, and the Verifiers library and Environment Hub for RL environments. Began as a decentralised-training project.

Our take

Supplier, not competitor; economics favour RL compute. Its Environment Hub is the closest thing among the clouds to a shared grader library, the evaluator problem approached from the training side.
