Summary

Extends textual-gradient prompt optimisation to multi-component, possibly cyclic LLM graphs with functional nodes such as retrieval, implemented in the AdalFlow library. A frozen backward-engine LLM produces feedback for each trainable textual parameter, sub-prompts are isolated, and gradients are computed selectively on error-prone samples.

Our take

TextGrad made practical for pipelines with loops; the same limitation of unverified textual gradients, in a small vendor's library.
