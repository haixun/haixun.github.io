Summary

YC W26 company that began as a real-time vision-language API and relaunched in October 2026 as an inference 'performance lab': it tunes a customer's self-hosted serving stack (vLLM or SGLang) with engine tuning, speculative decoding and custom kernels, claiming at least 50% fewer GPUs at the same latency SLA, and is paid only if the agreed target is met.

Our take

Optimises the physical plan of serving one deployment at a time, with outcome-conditioned pricing, a rare example of pay-if-target-met in this space, but delivered as services by a team of about five. Complement, not competitor.

Key numbers

  • At least 50% fewer GPUs at the same latency SLA (vendor-reported)
  • Gemma 4 attention kernel 1,770 to 464 ms on an RTX PRO 6000 (vendor-reported)
  • Multimodal preprocessing p90 428 to 81 ms (vendor-reported)
  • More than $5M saved for customers (vendor-reported, via press)
