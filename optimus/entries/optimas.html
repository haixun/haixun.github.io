Summary

Maintains one local reward function per component of a compound system, adapted to stay aligned with global performance, so heterogeneous configurations (prompts, hyperparameters, model parameters) can each be updated with their own optimiser while local gains translate into system gains.

Our take

A principled answer to credit assignment that mixes prompt, configuration and weight updates; its learned local rewards are an analogue of our per-step quality model, fitted rather than certified.

Key numbers

  • Average 11.92% improvement over strong baselines across five compound systems (author-reported)
