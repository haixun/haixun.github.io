Paper · 2024-10-14 · ETH Zurich · ICML 2025

A Unified Approach to Routing and Cascading for LLMs

author-reported

physical-plansearchestimategeneralcascades-routingcost-quality

arxiv.org/abs/2410.10347

People Jasper Dekoninck, Maximilian Baader, Martin Vechev

Summary

Derives an optimal cascading strategy, proves the optimality of an existing routing strategy, and combines both into cascade routing. Its analysis identifies good quality estimators as the critical factor in whether model selection helps.

Our take

Formal support for the claim in owning-the-intelligence that quality estimation, not the routing policy, is the hard core.
