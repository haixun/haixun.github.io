DemoEvolve: Demonstration-Guided Harness Evolution under Sparse Feedback

author-reported

harness-codesearchmetricgeneralharness-engineeringself-improvementevaluation

arxiv.org/abs/2605.24539

People Lirong Che, Yuzhe Yang, Peiwen Lin, Xu Cao, Chuang Wang, Xueqian Wang, Jian Su

Summary

Uses human demonstrations to guide harness evolution when rollouts are costly and feedback sparse and delayed; the coding agent extracts strategies from demonstrations and its own rollouts and turns them into harness components. Tested on turn-based games (Balatro, Slay the Spire 2, TextArena) with 30 paired logical seeds per comparison, a held-out split, and a mechanistic acceptance criterion requiring an auditable behaviour change.

Our take

The most careful acceptance protocol in the harness-search literature, and the clearest statement that a score movement can be seed variance. Its mechanistic criterion is worth borrowing.

Key numbers

  • Balatro mean capped final-round progress 16.83 → 20.00 on held-out seeds (author-reported)
  • Slay the Spire 2 mean floor reached 18.17 → 28.83 (author-reported)
  • 30 paired logical seeds per comparison, 60% used during evolution (author-reported)
