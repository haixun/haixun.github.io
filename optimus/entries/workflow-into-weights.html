Compiling Agentic Workflows into LLM Weights: Near-Frontier Quality at Two Orders of Magnitude Less Cost

People Simon Dennis, Rivaan Patil, Kevin Shabahang, Hao Guo

Summary

Compiles a procedural workflow into the weights of a small fully fine-tuned model instead of running an external orchestrator. Tested on travel booking (14 nodes), Zoom support (14 nodes) and insurance claims (55 nodes, 6 decision hubs), n=200 per condition, judged by two LLM judges with Wilcoxon, bootstrap CIs and Holm correction; it reproduces the finding that procedure-in-prompt dominates LangGraph-style orchestration.

Our take

The most dangerous paper for anything that emits an orchestration layer, and a demonstration of the comparator problem: the ranking between systems moves when the judge changes from Claude to GPT-4.1. Weights are a legitimate physical-plan target for stable procedures.

Key numbers

  • 128–462× lower cost per conversation; $50–80 one-time setup (author-reported)
  • Failure rate compiled vs LangGraph orchestrator: travel 5.5% vs 24.0%, insurance 9.0% vs 17.0%, Zoom 11.0% vs 9.0% (author-reported)
  • Insurance per-conversation cost: $0.327 in-context, $0.174 orchestrated, $0.0007 compiled (author-reported)
  • Orchestration frameworks exceed 290,000 GitHub stars combined (author-reported)
