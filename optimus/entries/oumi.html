Summary

Public-benefit corporation behind an open-source platform for training, evaluating and serving foundation models, now selling FAiE ('Frontier AI Engineer'), an agent that plans and runs evaluation, data curation, training, inference and monitoring. Deploys hosted, in the customer's VPC, on-premises or at the edge.

Our take

Partly the thesis: an agent that plans across models, data, evals and training methods. But it presupposes the fine-tune answer (no RAG product, no classical ML) and has to open by eliciting criteria and building an eval suite, which is the hard part.

Key numbers

  • Invoice-validation model at 99% validity, ~100x cheaper than GPT-5.2 (vendor-reported case study)
  • Text-to-SQL model matching Qwen3-235B at ~10x lower cost (vendor-reported case study)
