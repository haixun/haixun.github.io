Research catalog · November 2026

Optimus

An enterprise AI task is a program — business logic and data with model calls at a few points — and the interesting question is how to optimise and certify that program, not any one model. This catalog organises the companies, papers, products, articles and benchmarks working on that question: prompt and program optimisers, workflow search and compilation, semantic-operator optimisers from the database world, harness engineering, and the commercial attempts at “describe the task, we optimise it”.

Start here

  • Owning the Intelligence

    The thesis. The customer should own the logical program and its definition of correct; an optimiser regenerates the physical plan — which model, prompt, cascade or code runs each step — and certifies it. Why that is harder than query optimisation, what exists, what the earlier attempts show, and what would kill it.

    EssayNovember 2026

  • The Landscape

    The map: compile versus search, three research schools, four commercial tiers, the earlier attempts, and the layer that is empty everywhere — a trustworthy answer to whether the output is right.
