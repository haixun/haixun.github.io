Objectives

  • Understand sentence 6, with its two descriptive asides before the main verb.
  • Recognise three irregular or reflexive preterites: se presentó, hizo, and the imperfect llamaba.
  • Use lo que (what) and él mismo (he himself).

The main statement of this sentence is simple: Un gitano ... hizo una ... demostración, "a gypsy put on a demonstration". Everything else is decoration hung on that frame. Find the frame first and the long sentence becomes easy.

Today's text

6

Un gitano corpulento, de barba montaraz y manos de gorrión, que se presentó con el nombre de Melquíades, hizo una truculenta demostración pública de lo que él mismo llamaba la octava maravilla de los sabios alquimistas de Macedonia.

A heavy gypsy with an untamed beard and sparrow hands, who introduced himself as Melquíades, put on a bold public demonstration of what he himself called the eighth wonder of the learned alchemists of Macedonia.

Grammar and expressions

The frame and its asides

Un gitano corpulento | aside 1: de barba montaraz y manos de gorrión | aside 2: que se presentó con el nombre de Melquíades | hizo una truculenta demostración pública | de lo que él mismo llamaba la octava maravilla...

The commas in the text mark the asides exactly.

Describing people with de

de barba montaraz y manos de gorrión: "of wild beard and sparrow hands". Spanish describes a person's features with de where English uses "with". mano is feminine even though it ends in -o.

se presentó

presentar is "to introduce"; with se the action turns back on the doer: se presentó, "he introduced himself". The -ó ending is the preterite you met in llevó.

hizo

The preterite of hacer (to do, to make), and irregular: hizo, "he made". hacer una demostración is to give or put on a demonstration.

truculenta: a false friend

It does not mean "truculent". In Spanish it means lurid, sensational, calculated to shock. It stands before the noun here; an adjective placed in front carries the speaker's attitude rather than a plain fact. pública, the plain fact, comes after.

lo que él mismo llamaba

lo que means "what" in the sense of "the thing that". él with an accent is "he" (without the accent, el is "the"). mismo after a pronoun adds "-self": él mismo, "he himself". llamaba is imperfect because it was his habitual name for it.

la octava maravilla

octava is "eighth", feminine to match maravilla (wonder). los sabios alquimistas: "the learned alchemists"; alquimista ends in -a but is masculine here.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Names and hard words

  • Melquíades: four syllables, mel-KEE-a-des. The qu is k; the accent keeps í and a apart.
  • gorrión: hard g, rolled rr, go-RRYON.
  • hizo: silent h, z as s: EE-so.
  • montaraz: ends in z, so stress the last syllable: mon-ta-RAS.
  • demostración pública: de-mos-tra-SYON POO-bli-ka.
  • llamaba: ya-MA-ba.
  • alquimistas: al-kee-MEES-tas.

Reading the asides

Drop your voice slightly for the two asides and lift it again at hizo. That is how a listener hears which words form the main sentence.

Memorise

Frame first. Learn the skeleton until it is automatic: Un gitano corpulento ... hizo una truculenta demostración pública. Then add one aside at a time: first the beard and hands, then the name, then the tail about the eighth wonder.
