Nearly burning the house, and the manual

Objectives

A short sentence of domestic danger, then a long one of solitary study that ends in a triumph on paper: a manual.

Today's text

15

Ante las protestas de su mujer, alarmada por tan peligrosa inventiva, estuvo a punto de incendiar la casa.

Over the protests of his wife, who was alarmed at such a dangerous invention, at one point he was ready to set the house on fire.

16

Pasaba largas horas en su cuarto, haciendo cálculos sobre las posibilidades estratégicas de su arma novedosa, hasta que logró componer un manual de una asombrosa claridad didáctica y un poder de convicción irresistible.

He would spend hours on end in his room, calculating the strategic possibilities of his novel weapon until he succeeded in putting together a manual of startling instructional clarity and an irresistible power of conviction.

Grammar and expressions

Ante las protestas de su mujer

ante is "before", "in the face of". It appears again in ante los poderes militares and ante Melquíades.

alarmada por tan peligrosa inventiva

alarmada is feminine because it describes su mujer. tan + adjective + noun, with no que, means "such": "such dangerous inventiveness".

estuvo a punto de incendiar la casa

Spanish has two verbs "to be". ser (era, fue, es) says what something is. estar says where or in what state it is. estuvo is the preterite of estar. estar a punto de + infinitive is "to be on the point of", "to come close to". You will read it three times in this paragraph.

Pasaba largas horas

pasar + time is "to spend". Imperfect: he would do this day after day. largas means long, not large.

haciendo cálculos

The gerund of -er and -ir verbs ends in -iendo: hacer → haciendo, beside -ando for -ar verbs (arrastrando).

hasta que logró componer un manual

hasta que, "until". logró + infinitive you know from lo único que logró desenterrar: then an armour, now a book.

Two phrases describing the manual

de una asombrosa claridad didáctica and (de) un poder de convicción irresistible. Each noun has a colouring adjective in front or a plain one behind: astonishing instructional clarity; an irresistible power of persuasion. asombrosa is the adjective used for the demonstration in sentence 7.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Sounds to watch

  • peligrosa: pe-lee-GRO-sa.
  • estuvo: es-TOO-bo.
  • incendiar: een-sen-DYAR.
  • haciendo: silent h, a-SYEN-do.
  • cálculos, estratégicas, didáctica: each has its written accent on the stressed syllable.
  • posibilidades: six even syllables, po-see-bee-lee-DA-des.
  • convicción: cc is "ks", kon-beek-SYON.

Long adjectives in a row

posibilidades estratégicas, claridad didáctica, convicción irresistible: build each pair from its last syllable backwards, as in Lesson 8.
