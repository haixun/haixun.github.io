Grammar and expressions

volvieron, exhibieron, sentaron, instalaron

Four completed actions by "they". In paragraph 1 you met -aron (llevaron, lograron), the ending for -ar verbs. Verbs in -er and -ir take -ieron: volver → volvieron (they came back), exhibir → exhibieron (they exhibited).

Preterite-ar verbs-er / -ir verbs
he, she, it-ó: llevó-ió: consiguió
they-aron: sentaron-ieron: volvieron

llevaban

Imperfect, among all those preterites: it describes what they had with them when they arrived. It is the fourth form of llevar you have met.

Esta vez

esta is "this", for what is near; aquella was "that", far away. vez is "time" in the sense of an occasion: esta vez, "this time".

una lupa del tamaño de un tambor

del tamaño de: "of the size of". Spanish needs both de's where English says simply "the size of a drum".

el último descubrimiento

último before a noun means "latest". Compare the sales talk in paragraph 1, la octava maravilla de los sabios alquimistas de Macedonia: the same structure, a new exotic source.

Sentaron una gitana

sentar is to seat somebody; sentarse, with se, is to sit down oneself. You will meet se sentó in the last lines of this paragraph. gitana is the feminine of gitano.

e instalaron

y (and) changes to e before a word that starts with the sound i, to keep the two from running together: e instalaron.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Sounds to watch

  • volvieron: both v's are soft b's, bol-BYEH-ron.
  • catalejo: j as breathy h, ka-ta-LEH-ho.
  • tamaño: ta-MA-nyo.
  • exhibieron: silent h, x as ks, ek-see-BYEH-ron.
  • judíos: hu-DEE-os, three syllables.
  • Ámsterdam: stress on the first syllable.
  • descubrimiento: five syllables, des-ku-bree-MYEN-to.

2What two things did they bring?
un catalejo (a telescope) and una lupa del tamaño de un tambor (a magnifying glass the size of a drum).
3Whose discovery did they say it was?
de los judíos de Ámsterdam: of the Jews of Amsterdam.
4Where did they put the gypsy woman, and where the telescope?
Her at one end of the village (en un extremo de la aldea); the telescope at the entrance of the tent (a la entrada de la carpa).
5Why e and not y before instalaron?
Because instalaron begins with the sound i.
6Give the "they" preterite of volver and of sentar.
volvieron; sentaron.

Review

Recite the last sentence of paragraph 1 (Cuando José Arcadio Buendía y los cuatro hombres...) and go straight into En marzo volvieron los gitanos. The two paragraphs should feel like one story.

Memorise

Arrive, show, set up. Three sentences, three actions: they came back; they brought and exhibited; they seated and installed. Sentence 3 is two matching halves: Sentaron una gitana + where, e instalaron el catalejo + where.
