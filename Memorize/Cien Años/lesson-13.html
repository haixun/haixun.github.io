Grammar and expressions

Úrsula Iguarán, su mujer

mujer means both "woman" and "wife". su mujer, between commas, tells us who Úrsula is: "his wife".

que contaba con aquellos animales

contar con = "to count on". Imperfect, because she had been relying on them all along. aquellos is the masculine plural of aquel.

para ensanchar el desmedrado patrimonio doméstico

ensanchar comes from ancho, "wide": to widen, to enlarge. The noun patrimonio is wrapped in two adjectives, one in front for the narrator's judgement (desmedrado, "meagre") and one behind for the plain category (doméstico, "household").

no consiguió disuadirlo

conseguir + infinitive = "to manage to". consiguió is its preterite. disuadirlo = disuadir + lo (him), attached to the infinitive as in mencionarlas.

«Muy pronto ha de sobrarnos oro...»

ha de + infinitive is the present-tense twin of había de in sentence 1: "is bound to". sobrar means "to be more than enough". nos, "to us", is attached to it. The subject, oro, comes after the verb. Literally: "Very soon gold is bound to be left over to us" – we shall have gold to spare.

para empedrar la casa

empedrar is em- + piedra (stone, from sentence 2): to pave with stones. He plans to pave the house with gold.

replicó su marido

Verb first, then subject, as in pregonaba el gitano. Here su means "her": su marido, "her husband". su can be his, her, its or their; the context decides.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Names

Úrsula: stress on the first syllable, OOR-su-la. Iguarán: gu + a is "gw", ee-gwa-RAN.

gu before i

In consiguió the u after g is silent; it is only there to keep the g hard: kon-see-GYO. Compare Iguarán, where gu before a is pronounced "gw".

Other points

  • mujer: j as h, stress on the last syllable, mu-HER.
  • muy: one syllable, mwee.
  • disuadirlo: dee-swa-DEER-lo.
  • ensanchar: en-san-CHAR.
  • doméstico: stress on mé.
  • replicó: stress on the final ó; rolled initial r.

Memorise

Objection and reply. Sentence 12 is her case, sentence 13 his answer. For sentence 12 keep the frame, Úrsula Iguarán ... no consiguió disuadirlo, and hang the reason inside it.

Sentence 13 is worth learning as a quotation you could use yourself. Say it with a confident smile: Muy pronto ha de sobrarnos oro para empedrar la casa.
