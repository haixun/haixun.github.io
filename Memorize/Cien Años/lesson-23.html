Grammar and expressions

Un mediodía ardiente

un + a time word means "one": "one burning noon". mediodía is medio (half) + día (day).

hicieron, pusieron

The "they" preterites of hacer and poner, both irregular. You know the singular hizo from hizo una truculenta demostración pública; here is its plural with almost the same object: hicieron una asombrosa demostración.

The colon

The colon after gigantesca announces the demonstration itself: what follows explains what came before.

le prendieron fuego

prender fuego a something is to set fire to it. le, "to it", stands for the heap of dry grass.

mediante la concentración de los rayos solares

mediante, "by means of", for the second time. This phrase returns word for word in sentence 14.

que aún no acababa de consolarse

aún with an accent is "still, yet". no acabar de + infinitive is "not quite to manage to": he had still not quite got over it. Without the accent, aun is "even", as in aun los objetos perdidos.

concibió la idea de utilizar

la idea de + infinitive: "the idea of using". aquel invento echoes aquella invención inútil in paragraph 1.

un arma de guerra

arma is feminine, but like el ánima it begins with a stressed a-, so it takes un and el in the singular. Its adjective stays feminine: su arma novedosa in sentence 16.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Sounds to watch

  • mediodía: four syllables, me-dyo-DEE-a.
  • hicieron: silent h, ee-SYEH-ron.
  • gigantesca: the first g is a breathy h, the second is hard: hee-gan-TES-ka.
  • hierba: YER-ba.
  • calle: KA-ye.
  • fuego: FWEH-go.
  • aún: two syllables, a-OON, against one for aun.
  • guerra: gu before e is a hard g with silent u, and rr is rolled: GEH-rra.
  • concibió: kon-see-BYO.

4What did he want to use the magnifying glass for?
As a weapon of war: como un arma de guerra.
5aún or aun: which means "still"?
aún, with the accent.
6Why un arma when arma is feminine?
Feminine nouns that begin with a stressed a- take un and el in the singular.
