Today's text

13

José Arcadio Buendía no trató siquiera de consolarla, entregado por entero a sus experimentos tácticos con la abnegación de un científico y aun a riesgo de su propia vida.

José Arcadio Buendía made no attempt to console her, completely absorbed in his tactical experiments with the abnegation of a scientist and even at the risk of his own life.

14

Tratando de demostrar los efectos de la lupa en la tropa enemiga, se expuso él mismo a la concentración de los rayos solares y sufrió quemaduras que se convirtieron en úlceras y tardaron mucho tiempo en sanar.

In an attempt to show the effects of the glass on enemy troops, he exposed himself to the concentration of the sun’s rays and suffered burns which turned into sores that took a long time to heal.

Grammar and expressions

no trató siquiera de consolarla

siquiera with a negative is "even": "did not even try to console her". consolarla = consolar + la (her). Compare consolarse in sentence 8: he could not console himself, and does not console her.

entregado por entero a sus experimentos tácticos

entregado is a participle describing him: "given over", "devoted". por entero is "entirely". The whole phrase hangs on the main clause the way an "-ed" phrase does in English.

con la abnegación de un científico

"With the self-denial of a scientist". The irony is the narrator's: his science is setting himself on fire.

y aun a riesgo de su propia vida

aun without an accent: "even". a riesgo de, "at the risk of". propia before the noun means "own": su propia vida, "his own life". (After the noun it meant "of their own": vida propia.)

se expuso él mismo

expuso is the irregular preterite of exponer, formed like poner → puso. se makes it reflexive and él mismo underlines it: "he exposed himself, in person".

quemaduras que se convirtieron en úlceras

convertirse en: "to turn into". quemadura comes from quemar, to burn; the ending -dura makes the result of an action.

tardaron mucho tiempo en sanar

tardar + a length of time + en + infinitive: "to take that long to". The burns are the subject: they "took a long time to heal". mucho tiempo you know from desde hacía mucho tiempo.

Memorise

Cause and consequence. Sentence 14 is a chain: trying to demonstrate → he exposed himself → he suffered burns → they became sores → they were slow to heal. Each link causes the next.
