Grammar and expressions

Fue esa la época en que...

fue = "was" (from ser). esa is "that", the middle distance between esta and aquella. Putting it before the noun phrase is emphatic: "That was the time when...". en que after a time word, as in aquella tarde remota en que.

adquirió el hábito de hablar a solas

el hábito de + infinitive. a solas, "alone"; hablar a solas is to talk to oneself.

paseándose por la casa

The gerund of pasearse with se attached. Adding a syllable would shift the stress, so a written accent holds it in place: paseando → paseándose.

sin hacer caso de nadie

hacer caso de, "to pay attention to". After sin, Spanish uses the negative word: literally "without paying attention to nobody".

mientras Úrsula y los niños se partían el espinazo

mientras, "while", with the imperfect for what was going on in the background. partirse el espinazo, "to break one's back": the body part takes el, as in con el dedo.

cuidando... and six crops

cuidar is "to look after"; you met the noun in al cuidado de. Then three pairs, each joined by y: el plátano y la malanga, la yuca y el ñame, la ahuyama y la berenjena. Spanish names each crop in the singular with its article.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

The crops

  • plátano: stress on the first syllable, PLA-ta-no.
  • malanga: ma-LAN-ga.
  • yuca: YOO-ka.
  • ñame: NYA-me.
  • ahuyama: silent h, a-u-YA-ma.
  • berenjena: j as h, be-ren-HEH-na.

Other points

  • época, hábito: accent on the first syllable; the h of hábito and hablar is silent.
  • adquirió: ad-kee-RYO.
  • paseándose: pa-se-AN-do-se.
  • espinazo: es-pee-NA-so.
  • huerta: WER-ta.

Memorise

The hinge is mientras. Before it: the time, the habit, the pacing, the ignoring. After it: who, doing what, where, and the six crops.

The crops alternate gender within each pair: el ... la, la ... el, la ... la. Say them as three pairs with a small pause between.
