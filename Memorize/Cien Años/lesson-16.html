The armour

All that effort, and what comes out of the ground is not gold but a rusted suit of armour. The sentence is built to disappoint: Lo único..., "the only thing...".

Today's text

16

Lo único que logró desenterrar fue una armadura del siglo XV con todas sus partes soldadas por un cascote de óxido, cuyo interior tenía la resonancia hueca de un enorme calabazo lleno de piedras.

The only thing he succeeded in doing was to unearth a suit of fifteenth-century armor which had all of its pieces soldered together with rust and inside of which there was the hollow resonance of an enormous stone-filled gourd.

Grammar and expressions

Lo único que logró desenterrar

lo + adjective makes an abstract "the ... thing": lo único, "the only thing". lograr + infinitive is "to manage to", "to succeed in"; there is no preposition between the two verbs. desenterrar = des- + en- + tierra: to un-earth. That is the third des- verb of digging and pulling out.

fue: "was"

In sentence 7 fue meant "went". Here it means "was". The verbs ir (to go) and ser (to be) share their whole preterite. Context always tells them apart: fue de casa en casa (went), fue una armadura (was).

del siglo XV

Centuries are written in Roman numerals and read as ordinary numbers: siglo quince, "century fifteen". With de + el: del siglo XV.

con todas sus partes soldadas por un cascote de óxido

todas sus partes, "all its parts": todas and soldadas are feminine plural to match partes. por here is the "by" that follows a participle: welded by a crust of rust.

cuyo interior tenía...

cuyo, "whose", is masculine this time because interior is masculine (compare cuya imaginación). tenía is the imperfect of tener, "had".

la resonancia hueca de un enorme calabazo lleno de piedras

"The hollow resonance of an enormous gourd full of stones." lleno de = "full of". And there are piedras again, as in the riverbed of sentence 2, with enorme beside them once more.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Stress on the third-from-last syllable

Three words here carry an accent on the third syllable from the end: único OO-nee-ko, óxido OK-see-do, and earlier mágicos, metálicos. Such words always have a written accent, so you can never miss them.

x between vowels

In óxido x is a clear "ks".

Memorise

Outside, then inside. The sentence moves from the outside of the armour to the inside: found it (Lo único que logró desenterrar fue una armadura del siglo XV) → its surface (con todas sus partes soldadas por un cascote de óxido) → its inside (cuyo interior tenía la resonancia hueca...) → the sound (de un enorme calabazo lleno de piedras).
