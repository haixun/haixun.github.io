A world without names

Objectives

  • Understand sentence 3 and the pattern tan ... que (so ... that).
  • Recognise object pronouns attached to the end of an infinitive: mencionarlas, señalarlas.
  • Tell apart había de (was to) and había que (it was necessary to).

A short sentence with a big idea: the world is so new that things have no names yet. It is also a compact lesson in how Spanish hooks pronouns onto verbs.

El mundo era tan reciente, que muchas cosas carecían de nombre, y para mencionarlas había que señalarlas con el dedo.

The world was so recent that many things lacked names, and in order to indicate them it was necessary to point.

Grammar and expressions

tan reciente, que...

tan + adjective + que is "so ... that": El mundo era tan reciente, que muchas cosas carecían de nombre.

carecían de nombre

carecer de means "to lack". The verb always brings de with it, and no article is needed: carecían de nombre, "lacked a name". The ending -ían is the imperfect "they" form of -er and -ir verbs, the partner of -aban for -ar verbs.

-ar verbs-er / -ir verbs
he, she, it-aba: plantaba-ía: había, tenía
they-aban: precipitaban-ían: carecían

para + infinitive

para before an infinitive means "in order to": para mencionarlas, "in order to mention them".

Pronouns glued to the infinitive

las here means "them", standing for las cosas (feminine plural). With an infinitive, the pronoun is attached to the end and written as one word: mencionar + las → mencionarlas; señalar + las → señalarlas. With a conjugated verb it goes in front instead, as in lo llevó.

había que

haber que + infinitive expresses a need with no particular person in mind: había que señalarlas, "one had to point at them". Do not confuse it with había de recordar in sentence 1: de points to destiny, que to necessity.

con el dedo

With parts of the body Spanish says "the", not "my" or "one's": con el dedo, "with the finger".

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Sounds to watch

  • reciente: rolled initial r; c before i is s (or th): rre-SYEN-te.
  • carecían: four syllables, ka-re-SEE-an.
  • señalarlas: ñ again, se-nya-LAR-las.
  • dedo: a d between vowels is very soft, almost the th of "this": DEH-tho.
  • había que: a-BEE-a ke.

The comma is a breath

The narrator pauses after reciente. Use that pause; then take y para mencionarlas había que señalarlas con el dedo in one flowing breath.

Memorise

Cause, then two matching verbs. The sentence is a small argument: so recent → no names → to mention → to point. The last two verbs rhyme, mencionarlas and señalarlas, and they come in logical order: first what you want to do, then how you must do it.

Say the sentence while actually pointing at something with your finger on con el dedo. Gestures make a strong memory hook.
