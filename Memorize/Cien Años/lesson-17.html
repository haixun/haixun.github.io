What was inside

Objectives

The paragraph ends on a small, tender detail: inside the armour a skeleton, and at its neck a locket with a curl of a woman's hair. After all the noise of the magnets, the last words are quiet.

Cuando José Arcadio Buendía y los cuatro hombres de su expedición lograron desarticular la armadura, encontraron dentro un esqueleto calcificado que llevaba colgado en el cuello un relicario de cobre con un rizo de mujer.

When José Arcadio Buendía and the four men of his expedition managed to take the armor apart, they found inside a calcified skeleton with a copper locket containing a woman’s hair around its neck.

Grammar and expressions

Cuando ... lograron desarticular la armadura

cuando = "when". The subject is plural, José Arcadio Buendía and the four men, so the verb takes the plural preterite ending -aron: lograron, "they managed". Compare logró in sentence 16, when he was alone. desarticular = des- + articular: to take apart at the joints.

los cuatro hombres de su expedición

hombres is the plural of hombre (sentence 10). His gold hunt has become an "expedition" with a team of four.

encontraron dentro un esqueleto calcificado

encontraron: "they found", the same -aron ending. dentro = "inside". calcificado is one more participle used as an adjective.

que llevaba colgado en el cuello

llevar returns for the third time: "took" (1), "brought" (5), and now "wore". llevaba is imperfect, a state. llevar + participle describes what someone has on them: llevaba colgado ... un relicario, "had a locket hanging". colgado agrees with relicario. And en el cuello, "at the neck": the article for a body part, as in con el dedo.

un relicario de cobre con un rizo de mujer

Two more de phrases: de cobre, made of copper; un rizo de mujer, a woman's curl. With no article, de mujer names the kind of curl rather than a particular woman. This mujer means "woman"; in sentence 12 it meant "wife".

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

cu + vowel is "kw"

cuando KWAN-do, cuatro KWA-tro, cuello KWEH-yo. Each cu + vowel is a single syllable.

Other points

  • expedición: eks-pe-dee-SYON.
  • esqueleto: qu as k, es-ke-LEH-to.
  • calcificado: five even syllables.
  • llevaba: yeh-BA-ba.
  • relicario, rizo: rolled initial r; rre-lee-KA-ryo, RREE-so.
  • mujer: mu-HER.

1Who took the armour apart?

2What did they find inside?
un esqueleto calcificado: a calcified skeleton.
3What was it wearing, and where?
A copper locket hanging at its neck: llevaba colgado en el cuello un relicario de cobre.
4What was in the locket?
un rizo de mujer: a curl of a woman's hair.
5Why lograron here and logró in sentence 16?
The subject is now plural (he and four men): the ending -aron is "they", -ó is "he".
6Give the three meanings of llevar in this paragraph.
To take (lo llevó), to bring (llevaron el imán), to wear (llevaba colgado).

Memorise

A zoom to one curl. Like sentence 2, this one zooms in: the men → the armour → a skeleton → its neck → a locket → a curl. Each step is smaller than the last.

Learn the final three phrases first, as in Lesson 1, and work backwards: the ending is what an audience remembers, so it should be the part you know best.
