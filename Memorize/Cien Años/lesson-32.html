Grammar and expressions

De su puño y letra

An idiom: "of his fist and handwriting", that is, in his own hand. The subject of escribió is still Melquíades.

una apretada síntesis de los estudios del monje Hermann

apretada, "tight", "compact", stands before the noun for colour. síntesis is feminine. del = de + el.

que dejó a su disposición

dejó again, from sentence 20. a su disposición: "at his disposal", José Arcadio's.

para que pudiera servirse de...

para + infinitive is "in order to" when the same person does both things. When someone else is to do the second thing, Spanish says para que + subjunctive: Melquíades left the notes so that José Arcadio could use the instruments. pudiera is the subjunctive of poder; compare podrá, its future. And servirse de is what he wanted to do with the magnets in paragraph 1.

del astrolabio, la brújula y el sextante

Three instruments, each with its article. del covers only the first.

pasó los largos meses de lluvia

pasó, preterite: the whole season summed up as one event. In sentence 16 the same verb was imperfect, pasaba largas horas, for a habit. meses you know from paragraph 1.

encerrado en un cuartito

encerrado, "shut in", a participle describing him. cuartito is cuarto (sentence 16) with the diminutive -ito: a little room.

que construyó en el fondo de la casa

construyó is the preterite of construir; its participle, construidas, described the houses of Macondo. el fondo was the bottom of the river in paragraph 1; of a house, it is the back.

para que nadie perturbara sus experimentos

para que + subjunctive again. nadie is "nobody"; it needs no no when it comes before the verb.

1Who wrote the summary, and of what?
Melquíades, in his own hand: a summary of the studies of the monk Hermann.
2Name the three instruments.
el astrolabio, la brújula y el sextante: the astrolabe, the compass and the sextant.
3Where did José Arcadio spend the rainy months?
Shut up in a little room he built at the back of the house.
4Why did he build it?
So that nobody would disturb his experiments: para que nadie perturbara sus experimentos.
5Why para que pudiera and not para poder?
The person who is to use the instruments (José Arcadio) is not the subject of the main verb (Melquíades), so Spanish needs para que + subjunctive.
6What does -ito add to cuarto?
Smallness: cuartito, a little room.
