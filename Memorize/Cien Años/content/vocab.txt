# ---------- Expressions

+ frente al | frente a | compound preposition; a + el → al | facing, in front of the
+ pelotón de fusilamiento | pelotón de fusilamiento | noun + de + noun: Spanish builds compound nouns with "de" | firing squad
+ había de recordar | haber de + infinitive | a literary future-in-the-past: fate seen from the storyteller's distance | was to remember, would one day remember
+ en que | en que | relative phrase after a time word | when, on which
+ lo llevó a conocer | llevar a alguien a + infinitive | object pronoun before the verb; a + infinitive gives the purpose | took him to see (for the first time)
+ casas de barro y cañabrava | casa de + material | "de" + material says what something is made of | houses of mud and wild cane, adobe houses
+ a la orilla de | a la orilla de | prepositional phrase | on the bank of
+ se precipitaban por | precipitarse por | pronominal verb + por (movement along) | rushed down along
+ tan reciente, que | tan + adjective + que | result construction | so recent that
+ carecían de nombre | carecer de | the verb always takes "de"; no article before nombre | lacked a name, had no names
+ había que señalarlas | haber que + infinitive | impersonal obligation with no subject: "one had to" | it was necessary to point at them
+ con el dedo | con el dedo | Spanish uses "the" for body parts where English uses "my / his" | with one's finger
+ todos los años | todos los + plural noun | todos los / todas las = every | every year
+ por el mes de marzo | por + time | "por" with a time gives a loose "around" | around the month of March
