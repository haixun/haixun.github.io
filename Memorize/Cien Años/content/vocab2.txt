# ---------- Expressions
+ esta vez | esta vez | demonstrative + noun | this time
+ del tamaño de | del tamaño de | de + el → del | the size of
+ el último descubrimiento | el último + noun | último before the noun = the latest | the latest discovery
+ e instalaron | y → e | "y" turns into "e" before a word starting with i- | and (they) set up
+ a la entrada de | a la entrada de | prepositional phrase | at the entrance of
+ mediante el pago de | mediante el pago de | formal phrase | on payment of
+ la gente se asomaba | la gente + singular verb | gente is singular, so asomaba and veía are singular | people would look in
+ veía a la gitana | ver a + person | the "personal a": a direct object that is a person is introduced by "a" | (they) saw the gypsy woman
+ al alcance de su mano | al alcance de | a + el → al | within reach of their hand
+ ha eliminado | haber + participle | present perfect: ha (has) + participle in -ado / -ido | has eliminated
+ dentro de poco | dentro de poco | time phrase, literally "within a little" | before long, soon
+ podrá ver | poder (future) + infinitive | the future tense: infinitive + ending (-á = he / she / it will) | will be able to see
+ lo que ocurre | lo que + verb | "the thing that", as in paragraph 1 | what is happening
+ cualquier lugar | cualquier + noun | any at all | any place
+ sin moverse | sin + infinitive | English uses -ing here; Spanish uses the infinitive | without moving
+ en mitad de | en mitad de | prepositional phrase | in the middle of
+ le prendieron fuego | prender fuego a algo | "le" = to it (the heap of grass) | they set fire to it
+ no acababa de consolarse | no acabar de + infinitive | not to finish doing = not quite to manage | he had not quite got over
+ concibió la idea de | concebir la idea de + infinitive | noun + de + infinitive | conceived the idea of
+ un arma de guerra | arma de guerra | feminine noun with "un" because of its stressed a- | a weapon of war
+ otra vez | otra vez | literally "another time" | again
+ trató de disuadirlo | tratar de + infinitive | the same verb as "tratando de" in paragraph 1 | tried to dissuade him
+ terminó por aceptar | terminar por + infinitive | to finish by doing | ended up accepting
+ a cambio de | a cambio de | prepositional phrase; compare "cambió ... por" in paragraph 1 | in exchange for
+ lloró de consternación | verb + de + noun | "de" gives the cause of an emotion or bodily reaction | wept with dismay
+ formaba parte de | formar parte de | no article before parte | was part of
+ había acumulado | haber (imperfect) + participle | pluperfect: had done | had accumulated
+ toda una vida | todo un + noun | emphatic: a whole ... | a whole lifetime
