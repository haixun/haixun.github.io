Today's text

18

A pesar de que el viaje a la capital era en aquel tiempo poco menos que imposible, José Arcadio Buendía prometía intentarlo tan pronto como se lo ordenara el gobierno, con el fin de hacer demostraciones prácticas de su invento ante los poderes militares, y adiestrarlos personalmente en las complicadas artes de la guerra solar.

In spite of the fact that a trip to the capital was little less than impossible at that time, José Arcadio Buendía promised to undertake it as soon as the government ordered him to so that he could put on some practical demonstrations of his invention for the military authorities and could train them himself in the complicated art of solar war.

Grammar and expressions

A pesar de que...

"In spite of the fact that", "although". The clause it opens runs to imposible.

era en aquel tiempo poco menos que imposible

en aquel tiempo you know from paragraph 1. poco menos que, literally "little less than", means "little short of".

prometía intentarlo

Imperfect: he kept promising. intentarlo = intentar + lo, "to attempt it", the journey.

tan pronto como se lo ordenara el gobierno

Your first subjunctive. Spanish uses a special set of verb forms for things that are not facts: wishes, purposes, events that have not happened yet. The government had not ordered anything, so "as soon as it ordered him" takes the subjunctive ordenara, not the factual ordenó. For now, simply recognise the ending -ara (or -iera) as its sign.

se lo is "it to him": le (to him) changes to se when it stands before lo. The subject, el gobierno, comes last.

con el fin de hacer demostraciones prácticas

con el fin de + infinitive is a formal "in order to". Two infinitives depend on it: hacer and adiestrarlos.

adiestrarlos personalmente

los, "them", the military authorities. -mente added to an adjective makes an adverb, like English "-ly": personal → personalmente.

las complicadas artes de la guerra solar

arte is feminine in the plural: las artes. "The complicated arts of solar war", which exist only in his manual.

Durante varios años esperó la respuesta

Built exactly like Durante varios meses se empeñó... in paragraph 1. esperar means both "to wait for" and "to hope for", with no preposition.

1How hard was the journey to the capital?
Little short of impossible: poco menos que imposible.
2What did he promise?
To attempt it as soon as the government ordered him to.
3For what two purposes?
To give practical demonstrations of his invention before the military authorities, and to train them personally in solar warfare.
4Why ordenara and not ordenó?
The order had not been given; an event still to come after tan pronto como takes the subjunctive.
5What does -mente correspond to in English?
-ly.
6How long did he wait?
Several years: Durante varios años esperó la respuesta.

Memorise

Although, promise, when, why. Sentence 18: the difficulty (A pesar de que...) → the promise (prometía intentarlo) → the condition (tan pronto como...) → the purpose in two parts (hacer demostraciones, adiestrarlos).
