Nights under the stars

Objectives

  • Understand sentences 23 and 24.
  • Recognise habiendo + participle (having done) and hacerse (to become).
  • Follow a sentence that turns on three infinitives.

He abandons the household entirely, watches the stars, and learns to travel the world without leaving his room: the very thing Melquíades prophesied in sentence 6.

Today's text

23

Habiendo abandonado por completo las obligaciones domésticas, permaneció noches enteras en el patio vigilando el curso de los astros, y estuvo a punto de contraer una insolación por tratar de establecer un método exacto para encontrar el mediodía.

Having completely abandoned his domestic obligations, he spent entire nights in the courtyard watching the course of the stars and he almost contracted sunstroke from trying to establish an exact method to ascertain noon.

24

Cuando se hizo experto en el uso y manejo de sus instrumentos, tuvo una noción del espacio que le permitió navegar por mares incógnitos, visitar territorios deshabitados y trabar relación con seres espléndidos, sin necesidad de abandonar su gabinete.

When he became an expert in the use and manipulation of his instruments, he conceived a notion of space that allowed him to navigate across unknown seas, to visit uninhabited territories, and to establish relations with splendid beings without having to leave his study.

Grammar and expressions

Habiendo abandonado por completo las obligaciones domésticas

habiendo is the gerund of haber; with a participle it means "having done". por completo, "completely", is built like por entero. domésticas echoes el desmedrado patrimonio doméstico of paragraph 1.

permaneció noches enteras en el patio

permanecer, "to remain". noches enteras, "whole nights"; you met entero in por entero.

vigilando el curso de los astros

A gerund again, for what he was doing all the while. astro is any heavenly body.

estuvo a punto de contraer una insolación

The third estuvo a punto de. A man who nearly gets sunstroke by night-watching: the cause follows.

por tratar de establecer un método exacto para encontrar el mediodía

por + infinitive gives the cause: "from trying to". Then tratar de + infinitive, then para + infinitive: three infinitives in a chain. He wanted to find the exact moment of noon, hence the sun.

Cuando se hizo experto

hacerse + adjective is "to become". hizo alone was "made" in paragraph 1.

tuvo una noción del espacio que le permitió...

tuvo is the irregular preterite of tener; in the preterite it means "got", "came to have". le permitió: "allowed him".

Three infinitives after permitió

navegar por mares incógnitos, visitar territorios deshabitados y trabar relación con seres espléndidos: verb + noun + adjective, three times. deshabitados has the prefix des- you know: un-inhabited.

sin necesidad de abandonar su gabinete

Compare sentence 6: sin moverse de su casa. The prophecy comes true, in his imagination.

Memorise

Three and three. Sentence 23 turns on three verbs of his (permaneció, vigilando, estuvo a punto de); sentence 24 on three journeys (navegar, visitar, trabar relación). For the journeys, remember: seas, lands, beings, each with its adjective.
