Melquíades proves his honesty

Por último, cansado de esperar, se lamentó ante Melquíades del fracaso de su iniciativa, y el gitano dio entonces una prueba convincente de honradez: le devolvió los doblones a cambio de la lupa, y le dejó además unos mapas portugueses y varios instrumentos de navegación.

Finally, tired of waiting, he bemoaned to Melquíades the failure of his project and the gypsy then gave him a convincing proof of his honesty: he gave him back the doubloons in exchange for the magnifying glass, and he left him in addition some Portuguese maps and several instruments of navigation.

Grammar and expressions

Por último, cansado de esperar

por último, "finally". cansado de + infinitive, "tired of doing". esperar is the infinitive of esperó in the sentence before.

se lamentó ante Melquíades del fracaso de su iniciativa

lamentarse de something ante someone: to complain of it to them. del = de + el. fracaso returns from sentence 8, el fracaso de sus imanes: a second failure.

el gitano dio entonces una prueba

dio is the irregular preterite of dar (to give), one syllable and no accent. You met its imperfect in daban a conocer.

The colon, then two gifts

After the colon comes the proof itself, in two clauses that both begin with le, "to him": le devolvió los doblones (he gave him back the doubloons) and le dejó además... (and he left him as well...). devolver is de- + volver: to make something come back.

a cambio de la lupa

The same four words that ended sentence 10. The trade is undone.

unos mapas portugueses y varios instrumentos de navegación

unos is the plural of un: "some". mapa is masculine although it ends in -a. Nationalities have no capital letter: portugueses.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Sounds to watch

  • último: stress on the first syllable.
  • lamentó, devolvió, dejó: final stress; dejó is de-HO.
  • iniciativa: ee-nee-sya-TEE-ba.
  • dio: one syllable, dyo.
  • convincente: kon-been-SEN-te.
  • doblones: do-BLO-nes.
  • portugueses: gu before e is a hard g, por-tu-GEH-ses.
  • navegación: na-be-ga-SYON.

Breaths
