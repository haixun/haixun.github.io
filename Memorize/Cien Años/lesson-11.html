Grammar and expressions

cuya desaforada imaginación

cuyo means "whose". Unlike English, it agrees with the thing owned, not the owner: imaginación is feminine, so cuya, even though the owner is a man. Nouns ending in -ción are always feminine. desaforada, "unbridled", stands before the noun for emphasis.

iba siempre más lejos que...

iba is the imperfect of ir (to go): "used to go", "always went". You met its preterite, fue, in sentence 7. más lejos que: "farther than". Comparisons are más + adjective or adverb + que.

y aun más allá del milagro y la magia

aun, "even", as in sentence 7. más allá de is "beyond", and de + el fuses into del, the partner of al: más allá del milagro.

pensó que era posible servirse de...

pensó: preterite, "he thought" (one particular thought, on one occasion). era posible + infinitive: "it was possible to". servirse de: "to make use of".

aquella invención inútil

aquella again, as in aquella tarde remota. inútil is in- (not) + útil (useful). The narrator calls the magnets useless even as José Arcadio plans to use them.

para desentrañar el oro de la tierra

para + infinitive, "in order to". desentrañar contains entraña, "entrail": to pull something out of the guts of the earth. Compare desenclavarse: the prefix des- undoes or removes.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

Names

José: j as breathy h, stress on the accented é, ho-SEH. Arcadio: three syllables, ar-KA-dyo. Buendía: bwen-DEE-a.

g and j

  • imaginación: g before i as h, ee-ma-hee-na-SYON.
  • ingenio: een-HEH-nyo.
  • magia: two syllables, MA-hya.
  • milagro: hard g before r, mee-LA-gro.

Other points

  • naturaleza: five even syllables, na-tu-ra-LEH-sa.
  • inútil: stress on ú.
  • desentrañar: de-sen-tra-NYAR.
  • tierra: rolled rr, TYEH-rra.
  • allá: a-YA.

1What did José Arcadio Buendía think was possible?
To use the magnets to extract gold from the earth: servirse de aquella invención inútil para desentrañar el oro de la tierra.
2Why cuya and not cuyo, when the owner is a man?
It agrees with the thing owned, imaginación, which is feminine.
3His imagination went beyond three things. Name them.
el ingenio de la naturaleza (nature's ingenuity), el milagro (miracle) and la magia (magic).
4What two words make up del?
de + el.
5Which verb is the preterite of ir, and which the imperfect?
Preterite fue (sentence 7); imperfect iba (this sentence).
6Translate: más lejos que.
Farther than.

Memorise

Three steps up, then the plan. The aside climbs a ladder: farther than el ingenio de la naturaleza, beyond el milagro, beyond la magia. Then comes the plan in three infinitives: servirse (use) → desentrañar (extract) → el oro (gold).

Learn frame, ladder and plan as three pieces, then join them.
