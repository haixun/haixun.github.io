Grammar and expressions

Todos los años, por el mes de marzo

todos los + plural noun means "every": todos los años, "every year". por with a time is deliberately loose, "around": por el mes de marzo, "around the month of March". Months take no capital letter in Spanish.

Habit: plantaba, daban

Both verbs are imperfect, because this happened year after year: plantaba su carpa, "would pitch its tent". The subject is una familia, singular, so the verb is singular (-aba). By the end of the sentence the writer is thinking of the gypsies themselves, and the verb slips into the plural: daban, "they gave".

daban a conocer

An idiom: dar a conocer, literally "to give to know", means to make something known, to present it. You already know conocer from conocer el hielo.

un grande alboroto

Before a singular noun grande normally shortens to gran. Keeping the full form is an old-fashioned, storyteller's touch. Before a noun it means "great" rather than physically "big".

Event: Primero llevaron el imán

llevaron is the preterite "they" form of llevar, the same verb as llevó in sentence 1. Here it means "brought". The ending -aron (they, one completed action) contrasts with -aban (they, ongoing or habitual).

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

g before e or i

Before e or i, g is a strong, breathy h made at the back of the mouth: gitanos hee-TA-nos. Before a, o, u it is the hard g of "go": grande, gorrión.

z

Never the buzzing English z. It is s in Latin America and th in Spain: marzo MAR-so.

Other points

  • desarrapados: rolled rr, six syllables, de-sa-rra-PA-dos.
  • familia: three syllables, fa-MEE-lya.
  • nuevos inventos: both v's sound like b, NWEH-bos een-BEN-tos.
  • imán: stress on the accented last syllable, ee-MAN.
  • y con un grande alboroto: link grande alboroto into one chain.

1When did the gypsies come, and how often?
Every year, around the month of March: todos los años, por el mes de marzo.
2What instruments announced them?
pitos y timbales: whistles (pipes) and kettledrums.
3Why plantaba (imperfect) but llevaron (preterite)?
Pitching the tent was a yearly habit; bringing the magnet was one particular event.

They made known, they presented (the new inventions).

The magnet.
