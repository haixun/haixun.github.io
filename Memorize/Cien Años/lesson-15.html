Today's text

14

Durante varios meses se empeñó en demostrar el acierto de sus conjeturas.

For several months he worked hard to demonstrate the truth of his idea.

15

Exploró palmo a palmo la región, inclusive el fondo del río, arrastrando los dos lingotes de hierro y recitando en voz alta el conjuro de Melquíades.

He explored every inch of the region, even the riverbed, dragging the two iron ingots along and reciting Melquíades’ incantation aloud.

Grammar and expressions

Durante varios meses

durante = "during", "for" a stretch of time. meses is the plural of mes (month), which you met in el mes de marzo.

se empeñó en demostrar

empeñarse en + infinitive: to set one's mind stubbornly on doing something. The preterite sums up the whole period as one completed effort.

el acierto de sus conjeturas

acierto is "rightness", being on target. sus is the plural of su, used because conjeturas is plural. The possessive agrees with the thing owned, not with the owner.

Exploró palmo a palmo la región

exploró: preterite, "he explored". No word for "he" is needed. A palmo is a handspan; palmo a palmo is "inch by inch". The same pattern gives poco a poco (little by little) and paso a paso (step by step).

inclusive el fondo del río

inclusive = "even", "including". del = de + el. el fondo del río, "the bottom of the river": the same river of clear water as in sentence 2.

Two gerunds: arrastrando, recitando

He explored dragging and reciting. You know arrastrando from sentence 7, where Melquíades did the dragging. Now José Arcadio copies him.

en voz alta

Literally "in high voice": aloud. el conjuro de Melquíades, "Melquíades' spell", is his own phrase from sentence 8, recited now as a magic formula.

Four names for two iron bars

lingotes metálicos (7) → fierros mágicos (7) → lingotes imantados (11) → lingotes de hierro (15). Metal, magic, magnetised, iron: the wording cools as the hope fades.

Pronunciation

Respellings such as a-BEE-a are rough guides for an English speaker, with the stressed syllable in capitals. Trust the recording over the respelling.

x

Before a consonant, x is "ks" (often just "s" in relaxed speech): exploró eks-plo-RO.

Consonant to vowel linking

A final consonant jumps onto a following vowel: voz alta sounds like bo-SAL-ta, el acierto like e-la-SYER-to.

Other points

  • empeñó: em-pe-NYO.
  • conjeturas, conjuro: j as h, kon-he-TU-ras, kon-HU-ro.
  • región: rolled r, g as h, rre-HYON.
  • hierro: silent h, rolled rr, YEH-rro.
  • río: two syllables.

Memorise

Verb, place, two actions. Sentence 15 has a clean shape: Exploró + where (la región, el fondo del río) + how (arrastrando..., recitando...). Walk slowly across the room as you say it, miming the dragging.

Sentence 14 is three short beats: time (Durante varios meses), effort (se empeñó en demostrar), aim (el acierto de sus conjeturas).
