What you will be able to do

  1. Understand every sentence: each word, each expression, and how the grammar holds it together.
  2. Read aloud the whole paragraph with sound pronunciation and rhythm.
  3. Recite the whole paragraph from memory, knowing what you are saying.

How a lesson works

Each lesson takes 30 to 45 minutes and has the same parts: objectives, a portion of the text with audio, a phrase-by-phrase breakdown, new words, grammar, pronunciation, a read-aloud routine, questions with answers, a review of earlier sentences, and a memorisation exercise with a practice box that hides more words as you improve. One lesson a day is a good pace; the checkpoints (lessons 6, 10, 14 and 18) consolidate before you move on.
