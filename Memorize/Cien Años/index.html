The opening of Cien años de soledad

Understand it, read it aloud, and know it by heart. The course teaches exactly the Spanish these two paragraphs need: 1295 words in 47 sentences, 595 different word forms, taught in English, with the audiobook narrator as your model.

0 of 40 lessons complete Start lesson 1

What you will be able to do

  1. Understand every sentence: each word, each expression, and how the grammar holds it together.
  2. Read aloud both paragraphs with sound pronunciation and rhythm.
  3. Recite them from memory, knowing what you are saying.

How a lesson works

Each lesson takes 30 to 45 minutes and has the same parts: objectives, a portion of the text with audio, a phrase-by-phrase breakdown, new words, grammar, pronunciation, a read-aloud routine, questions with answers, a review of earlier sentences, and a memorisation exercise with a practice box that hides more words as you improve. One lesson a day is a good pace; the checkpoints consolidate before you move on. Each part ends with an assessment of all three goals.

  • ▶ buttons play the audiobook recording you supplied, cut to the sentence or phrase. You can slow it down and repeat it.
  • Voice buttons use your browser's synthesized Spanish voice. It is useful for single words and phrases, but it is not the audiobook and is less natural.
  • Tap any Spanish word, anywhere, to see its meaning, grammar, syllables and a dictionary link.
