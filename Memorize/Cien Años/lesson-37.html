Grammar and expressions

Los niños habían de recordar

The first sentence of the novel: el coronel Aureliano Buendía había de recordar aquella tarde remota. Here is the same construction in the plural, habían de recordar, "were to remember". One of these children is that colonel.

por el resto de su vida

"For the rest of their lives". su is "their"; Spanish keeps vida singular, one life each.

la augusta solemnidad con que su padre se sentó

con que, "with which". se sentó, "sat down": the reflexive partner of sentaron una gitana in sentence 3. su padre is "their father", and it too is in the novel's first sentence: su padre lo llevó a conocer el hielo.

a la cabecera de la mesa

cabecera comes from cabeza, head: the head of the table.

Two descriptions between commas

temblando de fiebre: a gerund, with de for the cause, as in lloró de consternación. devastado por la prolongada vigilia y por el encono de su imaginación: a participle with two causes, each introduced by por. su imaginación is the desaforada imaginación of paragraph 1.

y les reveló su descubrimiento:

les, "to them". descubrimiento appeared in sentence 2 as the gypsies' "latest discovery"; now he has one of his own. The colon hands over to his voice.

—La tierra es redonda como una naranja.

A line of dialogue set on its own is introduced by a long dash, with no closing mark. es, from ser, because roundness is what the earth is. redonda agrees with la tierra. como, "like", as in como huevos prehistóricos.

1What were the children to remember?
The august solemnity with which their father sat down at the head of the table.
2In what state was he?
Trembling with fever, devastated by the long vigil and by the fury of his imagination.
3What did he reveal?
His discovery: La tierra es redonda como una naranja, the earth is round, like an orange.
4Which sentence does habían de recordar echo?
The first sentence of the novel: había de recordar aquella tarde remota.
5Why es and not a form of estar?
Being round is a permanent characteristic, what the earth is, so ser.
6How does Spanish mark a line of dialogue set on its own?
With a long dash at the start, and nothing at the end.

Memorise

Frame, two asides, revelation. The frame: Los niños habían de recordar ... la augusta solemnidad con que su padre se sentó a la cabecera de la mesa ... y les reveló su descubrimiento. Inside it, two asides: temblando de fiebre, and devastado por ... y por ...
